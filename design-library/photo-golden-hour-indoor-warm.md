# Golden-hour indoor warm (rays through window)

Late-afternoon (or early-morning) sun streaming **through a window into a domestic interior** — bedroom, kitchen, living room, gallery, studio. Strong directional warm-yellow rays cast visible streaks across the floor or wall. Subject lit from one side with a golden rim-light, deep warm-shadow on the opposite side. **Distinct from `photo-warm-minimal-daylight`** (which is overcast or open-door — no directional sun rays) — this one is the SPECIFIC magic moment of sunset hitting an interior space. Domestic / intimate / nostalgic / verging-on-melancholy register.

## Visual signatures

- **strong directional warm-yellow rays** entering through window(s), casting visible parallelogram-shaped patches of light on floor / wall / bed
- subject lit from one side by the golden rays (rim-light catching hair, edge of cheek, garment edge)
- deep warm-amber shadow on the side away from the window — not black, never neutral; warm in shadow
- visible **dust motes / particles** in the rays (often), photographing the air itself
- warm color temperature (3000-3500K) but contrast is HIGH (direct sun, not diffused)
- interior props matter — bed / sofa / kitchen counter / record player / books on shelf — domestic context
- often shot at golden-hour just before sunset (lower-angle rays reach deeper into the room)
- composition often uses the rays as a graphic element (diagonal slash across the floor)

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: golden-hour interior, warm rays through window, directional sunset light, dust motes in rays, domestic golden-hour

**Lighting**: strong directional warm-yellow sun through window casting visible parallelogram light-patches on floor / wall, golden rim-light on subject, warm-amber shadow opposite, dust motes in the rays, 3000-3500K color temperature, high contrast (not diffused)

**Camera / lens**: Leica M6 with 35mm f/1.4 OR Canon 5D with 50mm f/1.4 — fast prime, slightly stopped down

**Film stock / post-processing**: Kodak Portra 400 or CineStill 50D — warm color science, gentle highlight roll-off, slight grain, retain the deep warm shadow rather than lifting

**Mood**: intimate, nostalgic, slow-time, on-the-edge-of-melancholy, domestic-magic-moment

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: overcast, diffused soft, cool tone, fluorescent, studio flash, outdoor environment, hard cool shadow

## Named references

**Photographers**: Petra Collins (warm-interior portraits, Coca-Cola "Petra" series, indoor-golden frames), Olivia Bee ("Bedroom Songs" series), Sophia Wilson (intimate domestic golden-hour), Ryan McGinley (interior work in portrait series), Tim Walker (golden-interior staged), Nan Goldin (some interior diary frames have this golden register), Theo Wenner (interior fashion)

**Magazines**: Apartamento (interior portraits — defining publication for this register), The Gentlewoman (at-home features), Cereal (interior pieces), Kinfolk (home stories with directional sun)

**Brands**: Glossier at-home, Aesop ritual-at-home shots, Lemaire home editorials, Universal Standard interior lookbooks

**Movements**: post-2015 intimate-domestic editorial, slow-living photography, at-home-portrait genre

## Example prompt template

> Indoor portrait of a person in a sun-warm bedroom (or kitchen / living
> room) in late afternoon, **strong directional golden-yellow sun streaming
> through the window**, casting a visible parallelogram patch of warm light
> across the wooden floor and partially up the wall. The subject sits on the
> edge of the bed (or stands at the counter), **half their face caught in
> the golden rim-light**, the other half in deep warm-amber shadow — not
> neutral, warm in shadow. Visible **dust motes drifting in the rays**.
> Shot on a Leica M6 with a 35mm f/1.4, on Kodak Portra 400, gentle
> highlight roll-off with slight grain, retain the deep warm shadow rather
> than lifting. Intimate, nostalgic, slow-time, on-the-edge-of-melancholy
> mood. Indoor golden-hour editorial in the Apartamento / Petra-Collins
> tradition.
