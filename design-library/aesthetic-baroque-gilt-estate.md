# Baroque gilt estate (aesthetic)

**Tag:** formal garden grandeur

**Canonical references:**
- Gardens of Versailles (André Le Nôtre) - axial vistas, parterre geometry, clipped hedge walls
- Louis XIV era engraved plates - gilt scrollwork cartouches framing every title
- Engraved bifurcated-serif display faces of the ornamental era - split-stroke capitals with flourish terminals
- Heritage maison stationery (embossed crests, gilt keylines on ivory card)
- Formal estate wayfinding - carved marble, gilt bronze lettering, crown finials

## Cultural identity

Sincere baroque formality - ornament worn without irony, apology, or a modern wink. The world is a choreographed landscape: deep hedge green and ivory in strict symmetry, gold scrollwork cartouches framing content the way a parterre frames a fountain, and engraved bifurcated serifs (split-stroke display capitals sprouting fine flourishes) announcing everything at ceremonial volume. Aerial views of formal gardens - geometric parterres, raked gravel, axial fountains - are the native imagery: landscape as ornament, seen in plan.

Where `aesthetic-neoclassical-remix` is DEFINED by a modern intrusion crashing the classical frame, Baroque gilt estate never breaks character - there is no glitch, no neon, no joke. And where `aesthetic-dark-academia` is worn, bookish and candlelit, this is groomed, horticultural and daylit: the estate at noon, not the library at midnight.

## Palette anchor

- Hedge green `oklch(35% 0.07 155)`
- Bosquet sage `oklch(62% 0.05 145)`
- Ivory `oklch(96% 0.015 95)`
- Gravel buff `oklch(89% 0.03 90)`
- Gilt bronze `oklch(74% 0.12 90)`
- Statuary gray `oklch(72% 0.01 250)`

Green and ivory split the page; gold is drawn, never poured. No black - the darkest value is hedge green.

## Decoration motifs

- Gold scrollwork cartouches and corner flourishes framing panels, cards and titles
- Engraved bifurcated-serif display capitals, letterspaced, with small flourish sprigs between words
- Symmetrical layouts on a strong central axis - the vista IS the grid
- Parterre-garden aerials and clipped-hedge textures as hero imagery
- Fine double-keyline borders (gold inside green, green inside ivory)
- Diamond and fleuron separators; crown or urn finials atop key panels

**Raster required:** the parterre-garden aerials and the gilt-relief scrollwork panels (the ornament frames can be SVG; the groomed landscape and carved gold cannot).

## Voice register

Ceremonial and horticulturally precise, with Latin permitted as ornament: "Axis et ordo." Announcements, not pitches - "The fountains play at four." Never exclamation, never abbreviation, never casual address.

## Failure mode

Ornament photocopied at low fidelity - a chunky clip-art flourish - reads as a wedding template, not an estate. Breaking symmetry for a trendy off-grid layout kills the whole premise; so does adding a single ironic modern element, which instantly converts the page into `aesthetic-neoclassical-remix`. Gold on white without the green goes bland-luxury; the hedge green is load-bearing.

## Best for

- Estates, châteaux, formal gardens, heritage hotels and museums
- Luxury weddings, invitations, patronage and membership programs
- Heritage food and drink houses (champagne, tea, confectionery)
- Classical music seasons, opera houses, formal cultural institutions

- Shells: `shell-centered-column`, `shell-hero-stack`, `shell-two-column-app`
- Styles: `style-serif-warm-paper`, `style-restrained-hairline`, `style-editorial-italic-accent`
