# German Expressionist woodcut

jagged knife-slashed figures and letterforms, one poison hue against black.

**Distinct from** `illust-saul-bass-cutout`: that is smooth mid-century scissor-cut paper with playful balance; this is violent gouged wood, every edge slashed at an angle, letters leaning and cracking, tension instead of charm.

## Visual signatures

- figures reduced to angular slashed silhouettes; faces cut into hard planes with wedge shadows
- hand-slashed letterforms: no curves survive, every stroke angled, widths jitter, baselines tilt
- exactly two inks: one acid or poison hue (acid yellow, blood orange, sickly green) plus pitch black
- rough tear edges, ink splatter, cheap-stock print grain; the violence of the cut is visible everywhere

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: German Expressionist woodcut, Weimar poster, knife-cut, angular, Die Brucke

**Material**: gouged woodblock, cheap poster stock, harsh offset ink, rough tear

**Line**: violent slashed cuts, wedge planes, jagged edges

**Color**: one poison hue on pitch black, two-ink print

**Style**: angular figure silhouette, leaning hand-slashed typography, cabaret drama

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: smooth curves, gradients, pastel, photoreal, clean vector, friendly

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Ernst Ludwig Kirchner, Karl Schmidt-Rottluff, Frans Masereel

**Products / films**: The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari posters, Der Sturm covers, Weimar cabaret bills

## Example prompt template

> German Expressionist woodcut poster for [CONCEPT], jagged knife-slashed
> angular figure silhouette, hand-cut leaning letterforms with no curves,
> two-ink print of acid yellow on pitch black, rough tear edges and ink
> splatter, Weimar cabaret drama, violent gouged woodblock texture.

## When to use

Nightlife and performance posters, protest and manifesto pieces, horror and thriller identities, bold countercultural editorial.

## When NOT to use

calm wellness brands, pastel kids products, corporate trust marketing
