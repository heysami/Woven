# Japanese woodblock print

carved-line mountain landscapes in indigo ink on warm washi paper.

**Distinct from** `illust-ink-wash-atmosphere`: that style is soft tonal wash with bleeding gradients; this one is crisp carved line, every contour cut by a knife into wood, with flat un-gradated color planes between the lines.

## Visual signatures

- carved woodblock linework: bold outer contours, fine parallel gouge lines for water, rock, and cloud texture
- deep indigo as the dominant ink, with sparse vermilion and sage accents on cream or kraft paper
- flat color planes with no gradients; tone comes from line density, not shading
- red circular hanko seal stamps and vertical calligraphic marks as compositional accents

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: ukiyo-e, Japanese woodblock print, carved line, mountain landscape, Edo period

**Material**: washi paper, kraft, letterpress ink impression, wood grain show-through

**Line**: carved knife line, bold contour, fine parallel gouges

**Color**: indigo dominant, vermilion accent, sage green, cream ground, flat planes

**Style**: layered mountain vistas, arched bridges, wave patterns, hanko seal stamp accent

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: gradient shading, watercolor bleed, photoreal, soft airbrush, neon

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Katsushika Hokusai, Utagawa Hiroshige, Kawase Hasui

**Products / films**: Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji, One Hundred Famous Views of Edo, vintage ekiben wrapper labels

## Example prompt template

> Japanese ukiyo-e woodblock print of [CONCEPT], carved knife-cut linework with
> fine parallel gouge texture, flat indigo ink planes on warm washi kraft paper,
> sparse vermilion and sage accents, red hanko seal stamp in the corner,
> layered mountain landscape composition, Edo period travel print aesthetic.

## When to use

Travel and regional food branding, artisanal product packaging, cultural editorial heroes, calm heritage identities.

## When NOT to use

glossy tech marketing, neon cyberpunk, corporate SaaS flat vector
