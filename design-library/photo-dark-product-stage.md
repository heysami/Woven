# Dark product stage

Single product spot-lit on dark organic staging — rocks, slate, velvet black — falloff into theater darkness.

## Visual signatures

- ONE product, hero scale, occupying 30-50% of frame
- Single sculptural key light (top-left or raking side), everything else falls to near-black
- Organic staging materials: volcanic rock, slate slabs, dark water, velvet, sand dunes at night
- Specular discipline: one highlight edge tracing the product silhouette
- Atmosphere: faint haze or mist catching the light cone

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: hero product on dark stone stage, single spotlight, black falloff, premium product photography

**Lighting**: one hard key with grid, faint rim kicker, no fill, light haze in the beam

**Camera / lens**: medium format, 80-120mm macro, f/8 product-sharp

**Film stock / post-processing**: digital clean, warm-black grade (never blue-black), deep shadow detail preserved

**Mood**: reverent, exclusive, unhurried

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: white seamless background, multiple products, colorful gradients, lens flare, busy props, daylight

## Named references

**Photographers**: product work in the Rolex / Audemars Piguet campaign tradition; still-life lighting after Irving Penn's dark plates

**Campaigns**: Nike Tier-0 retail product worship, Cartier Watches & Wonders staging, Apple Pro hardware films

## Example prompt template

> A single [product] resting on a slab of dark volcanic rock, photographed against pure theater darkness. One sculptural key light rakes from the upper left through faint haze, tracing a precise highlight along the product's edge; the rock's texture catches the beam and falls off into warm black. No other props, no fill light. Medium-format macro at 100mm, f/8, digital-clean with a warm-black grade and preserved shadow detail. Reverent, exclusive, unhurried product photography.

## When to use

Luxury-cinematic heroes (watches, jets, spirits, devices), premium drop pages, monochrome-pop posters (duotone-tint the result into the flood hue), dark SaaS pages that need one physical object.

## When NOT to use

Friendly/affordable positioning, catalogs needing many SKUs per frame, light pastoral or pastel registers.
