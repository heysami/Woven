# Stylized 3D self-avatar

A Pixar-grade stylized 3D character portrait of a REAL person - the designer/founder as their own hero asset.

## Visual signatures

- One character bust or three-quarter figure, stylized-real proportions (big eyes restrained, believable hair mass)
- Pixar/Fortnite-grade surfacing: soft subsurface skin, groomed hair clumps, fabric weave on the hoodie
- Personality props: headphones, glasses, a held tool (stylus, controller, coffee)
- Studio three-point light with a colored rim matching the site accent
- Friendly direct eye contact or candid mid-gesture pose

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: stylized 3D character portrait, Pixar-style avatar of a young designer, personable hero character

**Material**: subsurface skin, groomed stylized hair, knit/cotton fabric detail

**Line**: no line - full 3D shading

**Color**: natural skin/hair + ONE accent (hoodie or rim light) matching the page accent token

**Style**: feature-animation character lookdev, portfolio mascot register

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: uncanny realism, anime cel shading, low-poly, NFT-collection styling, generic metaverse avatar

## Named references

**Illustrators**: character lookdev in the Pixar/DreamWorks tradition, "Hi, I'm Jack" portfolio archetype, Fortnite icon-series fidelity

**Movements**: personal-brand avatar portfolios, vtuber-adjacent self-representation (3D, not anime)

## Example prompt template

> A stylized 3D character portrait of a young designer with curly dark hair and round glasses, three-quarter bust, wearing a deep-purple hoodie with visible knit weave, headphones around the neck. Pixar-grade lookdev: soft subsurface skin, groomed hair clumps, fabric micro-detail. Studio three-point lighting with a violet rim light, friendly direct gaze, slight smile. Feature-animation quality, personable, confident. Transparent background.

## When to use

Personal portfolio heroes ("HI, I'M JACK"), about pages, founder-led product sites, dev-tool marketing with a face, avatar systems for community products.

## When NOT to use

Corporate-formal brands, luxury registers, products deliberately avoiding individual attribution.
