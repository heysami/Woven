# Polish School poster gouache

dark expressive gouache where one symbolic image and hand-painted lettering share a single brush gesture.

**Distinct from** `illust-painterly-mythic-creature`: that style renders fantasy creatures with polished glow and depth; this is flat-dark poster gouache where the creature is a symbol, not a rendered subject. **Distinct from** `illust-gouache-storybook`: that is warm, friendly narrative gouache; this is melancholic, surreal and adult.

## Visual signatures

- dark expressive gouache strokes, dry-brush scumble left visible everywhere
- symbolic rather than literal imagery: one metaphoric figure (a howling silhouette inside a blood-red moon) stands in for the whole story
- hand-painted lettering integrated with the image - the title strokes are the same brush gesture as the artwork, slashed and tapering
- melancholic surrealist register: unease, poetry, negative space that presses in
- muted bruised palette: crimson, bruise violet, muddy ochre, bone, dusk on near-black ink
- rough poster stock texture, uneven ink coverage, no clean edges anywhere

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: Polish School film poster, expressive gouache, symbolic poster art, painted metaphor

**Material**: gouache on poster stock, dry ink, scumbled brush texture

**Line**: brush-slash strokes, ragged tapering edges, lettering painted with the image

**Color**: crimson, bruise violet, muddy ochre, bone, dusk purple, near-black ink

**Style**: single symbolic image, melancholic surrealism, figure-ground ambiguity, hand-lettered title as gesture

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: photoreal, glossy fantasy glow, clean vector, cheerful palette, literal movie still

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Franciszek Starowieyski, Jan Lenica, Wieslaw Walkuski, Wiktor Sadowski

**Products / films**: Polish CYRK circus posters, Polish one-sheets for western films

## Example prompt template

> Polish School film poster for [CONCEPT], dark expressive gouache with visible
> dry-brush scumble, one symbolic metaphoric image instead of a literal scene,
> hand-painted slashed lettering integrated into the artwork as the same brush
> gesture, melancholic surrealist mood, muted crimson, bruise violet, ochre and
> bone on near-black poster stock.

## When to use

Arthouse film and theatre identity, editorial covers on heavy themes, cultural festivals, anything that earns poetic darkness.

## When NOT to use

cheerful consumer brands, kids products, clean tech marketing
