# Mid-century duotone spot-color sleeve

Record-sleeve photography in the mid-century session tradition: a candid performance photograph rendered ENTIRELY in shades of one flood spot color - the ink does the lighting. Shadows go to solid ink black, midtones to a deep spot-color tint, and the whole sleeve is flooded edge to edge in the same hue. The photo is cropped hard into one corner or edge, leaving a large flat field where angled all-caps type blocks carry the design.

**Distinct from `photo-magnum-monochrome`** - that is neutral black-and-white documentary in natural gray tones; this one is a PRINT object: the photograph lives inside one flood spot color with halftone grain, hard crops and typographic architecture around it.

## Visual signatures

- one flood spot color (orange, cyan, acid yellow) covering the entire sleeve including the photograph
- photograph rendered as duotone: spot-color midtones + solid ink-black shadows, no neutral grays
- visible halftone grain and ink-press texture in the photo's midtones
- hard corner or edge crop - the subject is cut off confidently by the trim, never floating centered
- candid session subject matter: musician at the instrument, head down, caught mid-take
- angled or stacked all-caps type blocks (tight-tracked grotesque) locking into the photo's crop line
- generous flat color field as negative space - the emptiness is part of the composition
- tiny functional captions (date, venue, catalog number) in the same ink

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: duotone spot-color sleeve, jazz session photograph, flood spot color, hard corner crop, halftone print photo

**Lighting**: available-light session photography, single practical or window source, deep shadow masses that convert to solid ink black

**Camera / lens**: 35mm rangefinder or SLR with 50mm f/2, handheld, close working distance, caught mid-performance

**Film stock / post-processing**: Tri-X-style contrasty b/w capture converted to duotone - map midtones to the spot color, shadows to ink black, add halftone screen grain and slight ink-press unevenness, flood the ground in the same spot color

**Mood**: late-night session, confident economy, print-object physicality, cool restraint

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: full-color photography, neutral gray monochrome, soft pastel grading, centered symmetrical portrait, glossy digital cleanliness, gradients

## Named references

**Photographers / designers**: Francis Wolff session photographs, Reid Miles Blue Note sleeve design, Esquire-era duotone print features, Saul Bass poster economy

**Movements**: 1950s-60s jazz sleeve design, two-color offset printing culture, mid-century editorial print

## Example prompt template

> Record-sleeve composition flooded edge to edge in one spot color
> [ORANGE / CYAN], a candid session photograph of [SUBJECT] rendered as a
> duotone - spot-color midtones with solid ink-black shadows and visible
> halftone grain - cropped hard into the lower corner so the trim cuts the
> subject, large flat spot-color field above, angled tight-tracked all-caps
> type blocks locking into the photo's crop line, small catalog captions,
> ink-press texture, mid-century print-object feel.

## When to use

Music products and artist pages, playlists and session archives, poster-led campaign sites, editorial culture features, brands that want print-era confidence.

## When NOT to use

naturalistic product photography, soft lifestyle warmth, pastel wellness, clinical UI screenshots
