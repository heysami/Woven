# Push Pin Victorian psychedelic

Victorian circus display type paired with flat-color psychedelic flowing-figure illustration in a candy palette on cream.

**Distinct from** `illust-typo-art-nouveau-ornament`: that style is letterform ornament only - vines growing out of type with no figure; this pairs ornamental display type WITH flat psychedelic figurative illustration as one composition. **Distinct from** `illust-typo-house-industries-revival`: that is type-specimen revival lettering; this leads with the flowing silhouette figure, the type is its stage.

## Visual signatures

- Victorian and tuscan circus display type: split serifs, spurs, bifurcated wedges, stacked in alternating ink colors
- flat-color psychedelic figure: black silhouette profile with hair flowing out as smooth rainbow ribbon waves
- candy palette on cream stock: candy pink, teal twist, marigold, lilac haze, jet black
- ornamental frames and corner scrolls surrounding every composition like a playbill
- flat unmodulated shapes, zero shading or gradient inside a shape
- fleuron dividers, tiny stars and printers ornaments scattered as punctuation

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: Push Pin Studios style, Victorian circus display type, psychedelic silhouette figure, sixties poster revival

**Material**: flat ink on cream poster stock, matte print

**Line**: crisp flat edges, ornamental type serifs and spurs, no outline on shapes

**Color**: candy pink, teal, marigold, lilac, jet black on cream

**Style**: playbill ornament frames, rainbow ribbon hair, flat figurative silhouette, stacked two-color display lettering

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: photoreal, airbrush, gradient shading, grunge texture, dark palette

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Milton Glaser, Seymour Chwast, Push Pin Studios

**Products / films**: the Dylan rainbow-hair poster, The Push Pin Graphic

## Example prompt template

> Push Pin Studios style poster for [CONCEPT], black silhouette profile with
> hair flowing into smooth rainbow ribbon waves of candy pink, teal, marigold
> and lilac, Victorian tuscan circus display lettering with split serifs and
> spurs stacked in alternating colors, ornamental playbill frame with fleurons,
> flat unmodulated shapes on cream stock.

Music and event posters, editorial features, brand identities that want literate playfulness with vintage showmanship.

## When NOT to use

dark tech, brutalism, clinical dashboards, somber editorial
