# Arcane painterly 2.5D hybrid (Fortiche)

Netflix's *Arcane* (Riot Games + **Fortiche Productions**, 2021 / 2024) — the canonical reference for **hand-painted texture over 3D geometry**, the so-called **"2.5D hybrid"**. The signature is **manually painted textures (Photoshop) wrapped onto Autodesk Maya 3D models**, with painterly highlights, shadows, brushstrokes and imperfections baked into texture maps — every frame reflects emotional and artistic intent rather than procedural uniformity. The result reads as both 3D (volume, motion, depth) AND 2D (visible brushwork, painterly shading, hand-drawn line accents).

## Visual signatures

- 3D geometry (Maya) wrapped in **hand-painted texture maps (Photoshop)** — visible brushstrokes on every surface
- characters and environments share **the same painterly hand** — Piltover's walls, costumes, streets, faces, all painted to match
- **manually painted highlights and shadows** baked into texture maps (NOT procedural ambient occlusion)
- 2D-like line work and edge details integrated into 3D renders via proprietary shaders
- color palette of **rich saturated paints** — emerald greens, magentas, royal blues, brass golds (Piltover); industrial grays, hazard yellows, acid greens (Zaun)
- characters animated at **24fps with sometimes-12fps holds** for 2D-animation feeling
- compositing (Nuke) adds painterly atmospheric haze, color grading, smoke, glow
- everything looks like an animated *painting* — never plastic-render-clean
- silhouette emphasis — characters readable at any distance

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: Arcane Fortiche painterly hybrid, hand-painted textures over 3D, 2.5D animation

**Material**: 3D geometry with hand-painted Photoshop textures (visible brushstrokes), manually painted highlights and shadows baked in, painterly shading

**Line**: 2D-like line work integrated into 3D renders, edge details hand-painted, visible brushwork at silhouette

**Color**: rich saturated paint colors — Piltover emerald-magenta-royal-blue-brass, Zaun industrial-grey-acid-green-hazard-yellow

**Style**: painterly atmospheric haze, color-graded volumetric depth, silhouette-emphasized character design, 24fps with 12fps holds

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: procedural smooth-render, plastic CGI, photoreal, Pixar-clean toon, anime cel-shading, sharp clean vector, no-brushwork
