# Polaroid scatter pile

Instant-film prints scattered as physical objects - white frames, casual snapshots, overlapping at loose angles on a surface.

## Visual signatures

- Recognizable instant-film frame: white border, thicker bottom edge, slight square crop
- Snapshot content: flash-lit friends, pets, meals, places - candid, imperfect, warm
- Physical scatter: prints overlap at 3-15° rotations, soft contact shadows between layers
- Film character: slight cyan-green cast or warm fade, soft focus corners, occasional light leak
- Handwriting on borders (date, name, a heart) on some prints

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: polaroid photo pile, instant film prints scattered on a table, overlapping snapshots, white frame borders

**Lighting**: on-camera flash inside the snapshots; soft overhead light on the pile itself

**Camera / lens**: instant film (SX-70 / Instax character) for content; top-down 50mm for the pile

**Film stock / post-processing**: instant-film color shift (lifted blacks, cyan shadows, warm mids), matte finish

**Mood**: nostalgic, intimate, casually treasured

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: pristine studio product shots, perfect alignment, digital sharpness, HDR, single isolated print

## Named references

**Photographers**: Andy Warhol's Polaroid archive, Nan Goldin's snapshot intimacy (tamer register), Instax social culture

**Campaigns**: Gentle Rain parallax (polaroids drifting on dark stage), Urban Outfitters / Instax marketing

## Example prompt template

> A loose pile of eight instant-film prints scattered across a pale wooden table, overlapping at casual angles, each with the classic white border and thick bottom edge. The snapshots inside: flash-lit friends laughing, a dog on a beach, a birthday cake, neon at night - lifted blacks, cyan-tinted shadows, warm faded mids. Two prints carry handwriting on the border in ballpoint. Soft overhead daylight casts gentle contact shadows between the layered prints. Top-down, matte finish, nostalgic and treasured.

## When to use

Scrapbook-substrate heroes, memory/journal products, social apps with intimacy positioning, personal sites, event recaps, parallax photo-pile moments (each print a separate layer).

## When NOT to use

Enterprise software, clinical/medical trust pages, futurist tech briefs.
