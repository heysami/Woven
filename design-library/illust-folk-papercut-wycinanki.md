# Folk papercut wycinanki

symmetric layered paper cutouts of roosters and flowers with scalloped scissor edges.

**Distinct from** `illust-eric-carle-tissue-collage`: that is torn painted tissue with rough organic edges; this is crisp scissor precision, every edge deliberate, scalloped, and mirror-symmetric. **Distinct from** `illust-saul-bass-cutout`: that is abstract modernist drama in 2-3 colors; this is traditional ornamental folk symmetry, dense with motif and stacked in many bright layers.

## Visual signatures

- mirror symmetry everywhere: paired roosters flanking a central flower, motifs cut from folded sheets
- flat solid-color paper layered physically: navy under rouge under marigold under sage, each layer casting a hair of shadow
- scalloped and serrated edges as ornament; feathers, petals, and borders built from repeated cut notches
- traditional folk motif vocabulary: cockerels, tulips and roundel flowers, leaf sprays, scalloped border bands

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: wycinanki, folk papercut, layered paper collage, symmetric folk art, Lowicz

**Material**: flat gloss paper layers, crisp scissor cut, slight layer shadow

**Line**: no drawn line, cut edge only, scalloped serrated contours

**Color**: navy, rouge red, coral, marigold, sage green on parchment cream

**Style**: mirror symmetry, paired roosters and central flower, roundel medallions, scalloped borders

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: torn edges, gradients, photoreal, asymmetric chaos, muted grunge

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Lowicz papercut folk artists, Kurpie cutters, Polish wycinanki tradition

**Products / films**: Lowicz wycinanki roundels, folk festival posters, painted-cottage ornament sheets
