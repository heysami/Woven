# Amber cyber portrait

A figure in amber/tungsten low light wearing tech (AR glasses, headset, visor), face half-lit, near-black surround — the "modern architect of the future" portrait.

## Visual signatures

- Single warm key (amber/tungsten) raking one side of the face; the other side falls to black
- Worn technology as costume: AR glasses catching reflections, earpieces, visor edge-glow
- Tight portrait crop (chest-up), subject gazing past camera with intent
- Atmosphere: faint haze, practical bokeh points in the dark background
- Optional cool counter-accent (cyan rim from a screen) at 1/8 the key's strength

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: amber-lit portrait with AR glasses, tech-noir headshot, half-lit face against black, future-architect portrait

**Lighting**: warm tungsten key from 45°, deep falloff, faint cyan screen rim, light haze

**Camera / lens**: 85mm f/1.8, shallow focus on the eyes, practical bokeh behind

**Film stock / post-processing**: digital cinematic, amber-crushed grade, preserved skin texture, no HDR

**Mood**: focused, visionary, slightly clandestine

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: flat office lighting, smiling stock-photo energy, rainbow RGB wash, full cyberpunk neon street

## Named references

**Photographers**: Greg Williams' available-light celebrity work (warmer), Blade Runner 2049 portrait lighting (Roger Deakins), Apple Vision Pro press portraits (the polished cousin)

**Campaigns**: Viktor-O "Modern Architect" portfolio hero; gaming-hardware founder portraits

## Example prompt template

> A chest-up portrait of a designer wearing slim AR glasses, lit by a single warm tungsten key from the upper left: amber light rakes the left side of the face and catches a reflection in the lens edge, the right side falling into near-black. Faint haze, two soft practical bokeh points deep in the dark background, a whisper of cyan rim from an off-screen display. 85mm at f/1.8, focus on the eyes, amber-crushed cinematic grade with honest skin texture. Focused, visionary, slightly clandestine.

## When to use

Portfolio heroes for technologists/architects/founders, dark AI-product about pages, speaker/keynote promos, cyberpunk-adjacent briefs needing a HUMAN anchor instead of cityscapes.

## When NOT to use

Trust-forward corporate teams pages, healthcare, daylight lifestyle brands.
