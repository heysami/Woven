# Bioluminescent creature render

A translucent deep-sea or fantastical organism - jellyfish, medusa, glowing totem-being - self-lit against true black, tendrils trailing.

## Visual signatures

- ONE organism as the subject: jellyfish bell, siphonophore chain, glowing moth, totemic spirit-creature
- Translucent tissue with visible inner structure - veins, gills, cores silhouetted through the membrane
- Self-emission as the only light: brightest at the core/veins, exponential falloff into black water/void
- Trailing appendages: tendrils, filaments, fins - drawn out by implied current
- Sparse drifting particles (marine snow) catching the creature's own glow

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: bioluminescent jellyfish render, translucent glowing organism, deep sea creature against black, self-illuminated being

**Material**: subsurface-scattering membrane, emissive internal veins, wet-gloss outer film

**Line**: no line - glow gradients define form

**Color**: single glow family - cyan `#3ee8ff` / jade `#2effb4` / violet - on absolute black

**Style**: scientific-documentary meets fantasy creature design, render-grade translucency

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: ambient fill light, cartoon outline, rainbow multicolor, busy coral background, aquarium glass

## Named references

**Illustrators**: *Blue Planet II* deep-sea cinematography (the photographic canon), *Avatar* Pandora creature lookdev, Ernst Haeckel's radiolarian plates (the engraved ancestor)

**Movements**: deep-sea documentary aesthetics, biolum fantasy worldbuilding

## Example prompt template

> A single luminous jellyfish suspended in absolute black water: its translucent bell glows pale cyan from a bright internal core, fine emissive veins branching through the tissue, four long filament tendrils trailing into the dark below. The membrane's wet outer film catches its own light in one thin specular arc. Sparse marine-snow particles drift nearby, lit only by the creature. Render-grade subsurface scattering, exponential glow falloff, no ambient light, no background detail. Hushed, alive, rare. Transparent or black background.

## When to use

Bioluminescent-deep heroes (the static plate; the live pulse goes to the material/particle drawers), venture/deep-tech metaphor subjects, meditation-app spirit creatures, exhibition key art.

## When NOT to use

Friendly consumer mascots (use kawaii/plush registers), daylight brands, dense-information pages.
