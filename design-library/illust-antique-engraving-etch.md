# Antique engraving etch

dense crosshatched pen scenes at old printing-plate density, ink black on cream.

**Distinct from** `illust-noir-comic-ink`: that style is modern high-contrast brush ink with big solid blacks; this one builds every tone from thousands of fine parallel and crosshatched engraved strokes, like a 19th-century book plate. **Distinct from** `material-photocopy-xerox`: that is a degraded reproduction surface; this is the pristine hand-cut plate drawing itself.

## Visual signatures

- tone built entirely from fine hatching and crosshatching; no flat fills, no gray washes
- plate-print density: skies of ruled horizontal lines, foliage of stippled scribble, stone of curved contour hatching
- single ink black on aged cream stock, with faint plate-edge and uneven press pressure
- romantic subjects rendered with medieval-to-Victorian gravity: towers, ships, forests, ice, ruins

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: antique engraving, steel engraving, etching, crosshatch, 19th century book plate

**Material**: cream laid paper, plate impression, iron ink, uneven press pressure

**Line**: fine crosshatched strokes, ruled sky lines, stipple, contour hatching

**Color**: single ink black on aged cream, monochrome

**Style**: dense atmospheric scene, dramatic single subject, engraved illustration plate

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: flat fills, halftone dots, color, brush ink, photoreal, soft gradient

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Gustave Dore, Albrecht Durer, Victorian expedition-press engravers

**Products / films**: Dore's Divine Comedy plates, Harper's Weekly engravings, polar expedition newspapers

## Example prompt template

> antique steel engraving of [CONCEPT], dense fine crosshatched linework,
> ruled horizontal sky lines and stippled texture, single ink black on aged
> cream paper, 19th century book plate density, dramatic romantic composition,
> no flat fills, every tone built from hatched strokes.
