# Knit / yarn 3D character (cozy wool)

Characters and objects modeled to look like they're **knitted from wool, crocheted from yarn, or felted from raw fiber** — visible stitch pattern repeating across the surface, soft fiber-fuzz at the silhouette edges, slight stitch-row imperfections that read as "made by hand." Cozy / craft / slow-living register. Distinct from `illust-claymation-stop-motion` (clay surface), `illust-3d-fur-rendered-mascot` (simulated fur), and `illust-3d-plush-toy-soft` (fabric-stuffed with seams) — knit-yarn is FIBRE-STITCH with visible loops.

## Visual signatures

- visible **knit-stitch pattern** repeating across the entire surface (V-shaped purls, or Y-shaped knits, or interlocking crochet loops)
- soft fiber-fuzz at the silhouette edges (a few stray fibers)
- slight stitch-row imperfections — uneven tension, occasional dropped stitch — reads as "made by hand, not machine"
- soft warm palette — cream, oat, rust, sage, mulberry, butter-yellow (the actual wool-dye palette)
- pattern decoration via colorwork — Fair Isle bands, intarsia motifs, stripes
- accessories suggest the craft world — wooden buttons, cable patterns, bobbles, fringe
- studio-soft lighting catches the fuzz halo

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: knit, crochet, yarn, wool, felted, visible stitch pattern, hand-knitted

**Material**: knitted wool with visible V-shaped purl stitches (OR crochet loops, OR felted compressed-fiber), soft fiber-fuzz at silhouette edges

**Line**: no line; the stitch-row gridlines act as line

**Color**: warm wool-dye palette — cream, oat, rust, sage, mulberry, butter-yellow

**Style**: hand-knitted imperfections, occasional Fair Isle colorwork bands, wooden-button accents, fiber-halo at silhouette

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: smooth-surface, glossy plastic, hard-edge geometric, photoreal-skin, machine-perfect

## Named references

**Craft references**: traditional Fair Isle knitwear, Scandinavian Marius patterns, Japanese amigurumi crochet, Aran cable knits, Tilda fabric-craft aesthetics

**3D communities**: Sketchfab stylized-knit-PBR collection, BlenderKit knitted-fabric materials, Polygon Runway cozy-3D tutorials

**Brands / IP**: Animal Crossing villager style (the cozy 3D-character canon), Aardman knit-animation projects, Toast / Frame Mori cozy product photography

## Example prompt template

> Knit / yarn 3D character of [SUBJECT — animal / person / object], modeled
> as if **hand-knitted from wool yarn**, visible V-shaped purl stitches
> repeating across the entire surface, soft fiber-fuzz at the silhouette edges
> with a few stray fibers, slight stitch-row imperfections (uneven tension)
> reading as made-by-hand, warm wool-dye palette of cream-and-rust-and-sage,
> optional Fair Isle colorwork band across the chest, wooden-button accents,
> studio-soft lighting catching the fiber halo, cozy slow-craft aesthetic.

## When to use

Cottage-craft brands, slow-living wellness, cozy-tech, ASMR-adjacent content, parenting / nursery, craft-marketplace SaaS, autumn / winter seasonal campaigns.

## When NOT to use

cyberpunk, brutalism, B2B-tech, edgy fashion, fast-paced kinetic
