# Toyism symbol pop

hand-painted symbol-set pop: flat primary glyphs assembled from a shared vocabulary, with spray, brush and stamp paint textures.

**Distinct from** `illust-bauhaus-geometric`: that style is clean vector construction with mathematical edges; this is painted - the same geometry carries spray-edge stipple, brush-band stripes and stamp-grid texture. **Distinct from** `illust-shapes-gallery-michalczyk`: that is one artist's gallery abstraction; this reads as a collective language - a fixed symbol set recombined like signed sentences.

## Visual signatures

- flat primary geometric glyphs: stars, stepped blocks, circles, triangles, bars used as a fixed shared vocabulary
- shared-vocabulary composition: the same few symbols recombine into figures, cards and scenes, make-place-repeat
- spray-edge stipple: one edge of a shape dissolving into airbrush dots
- brush-band texture: diagonal painted stripe fills at 45 degrees
- stamp-grid texture: hand-stamped checker misregistration inside flat fields
- saturated three-ink palette on plate white: cobalt, magenta, lemon plus black ink accents; a hand-drawn collective signature mark

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: toyism style, symbol-set pop art, painted geometric glyphs, collective symbol language

**Material**: flat paint on white plate ground, spray stipple, hand stamp

**Line**: hard flat edges with one textured edge, no outlines

**Color**: cobalt blue, magenta, lemon yellow, black ink, plate white

**Style**: shared symbol vocabulary, stacked layered forms, playful ordered composition, signed-collective mark

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: gradients, 3d render, photoreal, thin line art, muted palette

## Named references

**Illustrators**: the Toyism art movement, Dejo and the Toyist collective

**Products / films**: Toyism painted plates and murals, symbol-language canvases

## Example prompt template

> Toyism symbol pop composition for [CONCEPT], flat primary geometric glyphs -
> stars, stepped blocks, circles, triangles - recombined from one shared symbol
> vocabulary, cobalt, magenta and lemon on plate white with black ink accents,
> spray-edge stipple on one side of each form, diagonal brush-band stripes and
> hand-stamped checker grid textures, playful ordered layering.

## When to use

Community and collective branding, playful data or system visuals, culture campaigns where a repeatable symbol language is the concept.

## When NOT to use

photoreal product marketing, luxury restraint, organic hand-drawn warmth
