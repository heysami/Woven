# Motorsport dramatic rig

Vehicle as sculpture under dramatic studio or track light — rig-shot motion, wet asphalt reflections, dust kicks.

## Visual signatures

- One vehicle (F1 car, GT sports car, truck, motorcycle) as the absolute hero, low 3/4 or head-on stance
- Dramatic single-source light carving the bodywork; wet-look ground reflections
- Motion language: rig-shot wheel blur, dust/spray kick, light streaks behind a static-sharp body
- Environment minimal: dark studio, night track, desert at dusk — never a busy paddock
- Optional livery typography interacting with frame edges

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: F1 car low angle dramatic light, rig shot motion blur, wet asphalt reflection, automotive hero photography

**Lighting**: single hard key + rim kicker, practical track lights as bokeh, smoke/haze in beams

**Camera / lens**: rig-mounted 35mm for motion plates; 85mm low tripod for stance shots

**Film stock / post-processing**: digital cinematic grade, controlled warm-cold split (amber key / teal fill), deep blacks

**Mood**: aggressive precision, kinetic, premium

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: showroom flat lighting, crowd backgrounds, fisheye, oversaturated HDR, toy-like rendering

## Named references

**Photographers**: automotive work in the tradition of Easton Chang, Amy Shore (heritage variant), GP Library rig archives

**Campaigns**: Porsche 911 launch films, Red Bull Racing content, Gran Turismo key art

## Example prompt template

> A scarlet Formula 1 car in low three-quarter stance on wet night asphalt, single hard key light raking across the sidepod and carving the front wing, amber highlights against teal-black falloff. Rig-shot energy: wheels motion-blurred, body razor sharp, fine spray kicking from the rear tires, light streaks trailing. Minimal dark track environment with two practical lights as distant bokeh. 35mm rig plate, cinematic grade, deep blacks. Aggressive precision, kinetic, premium.

## When to use

Automotive/logistics landings, racing and e-sports brands, monochrome-pop posters (duotone into the flood hue), speed-positioned B2B ("swift and simple transport"), product pages for anything with wheels.

## When NOT to use

Calm-tech, eco-first briefs (unless EV positioning explicitly wants restrained speed), family/safety-first messaging.
