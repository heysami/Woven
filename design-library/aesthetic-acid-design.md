# Acid Design / rave-flyer (aesthetic)

**Tag:** `aesthetic`

**Canonical references:**
- David Rudnick OPN sleeves — severe asymmetry, one chromed mark on void
- GUCCIMAZE Chrome Type — the melting-chrome wordmark vocabulary
- Jonathan Castro / Boiler Room — info-dense flyer language as identity system
- Heaven by Marc Jacobs — fashion sublabel using rave-flyer as luxury signal
- Berghain affiliate flyers — the manifest-as-marketing tone

## Cultural identity

Late-2010s revival of mid-90s European rave-flyer iconography, filtered through post-internet graphic designers (Rudnick, GUCCIMAZE, Castro) and absorbed by fashion sublabels and underground music labels around 2018–2024. The aesthetic descends from acid house / hardcore continuum flyers (Spiral Tribe, ESP, early Berghain) but rejects nostalgia — it reads as severe, insider, cryptic, never warm or ironic. The point is to look like you wouldn't get in.

Voice register: cryptic, info-dense, manifest-like. Venue / date / artist list reads as a system readout, not marketing. Lowercase or ALL CAPS, never title case. No exclamation, no "join us", no benefits-bulleted copy. Names are dropped without explanation; if you don't know the reference, you're not the audience.

## Palette anchor

True black ground — the wiki term is "colorful black" — never mid-grey, never gradient. Against it, ONE acid neon, picked and committed:

- Pure black `#000000` (ground; the canvas that makes neon vibrate)
- Acid green `#39FF14` (the canonical Spiral Tribe / hardcore green)
- Hot magenta `#FF1493` (the Heaven / fashion-sublabel pink)
- Electric cyan `#00E5FF` (the David Rudnick OPN-era cool)
- Hazard yellow `#F5FF00` (the warning-tape / Berghain register)

Plus a chrome / iridescent gradient reserved for the wordmark only — silver `#C0C0C0` through blue-violet `#6E00FF` to pink `#FF1493`. Never use chrome on body text or buttons. Mid-greys are forbidden — the contrast jump from `#000` to one neon is the entire aesthetic.

## Decoration motifs

Pick exactly ONE per composition, size it heroically:

- Wireframe globe / longitude-latitude sphere
- Barcode / EAN strip
- Smiley (the 1988 acid house yellow, but never coloured-in cute)
- Op-art checker fragment / Moiré field
- Tribal or celtic knot loop
- Halftone dot field

The buffet — every motif co-existing — is the dead giveaway of the cheap version. Canonical work is asymmetric, severe, and uses ONE chromed mark against ONE neon against true black.

## Failure mode

Every motif at once + Photoshop "Chrome" layer-style preset on Impact or Orbitron + rainbow holographic mesh-gradient ground + Inter body text + centered everything = AI rave cosplay. The tells are symmetry, abundance, and warmth. Real acid design is off-center, severe, void-grounded, and uses monospace for info — not Inter, not Helvetica, never Orbitron.

Also fatal: drop-shadowed buttons, soft glows, lens flares, generic SVG noise overlays, "fun" microcopy, title-case headings.

## Best for

- Club night / festival / mixtape landers
- Underground music label sites
- Fashion sublabel drops (Heaven-style, late-2010s onward)
- Zines, merch product pages, single-product hype drops
- Anything that needs to read as insider / scene rather than mass-market
- Manifestos, lineup announcements, archive pages for cultural institutions

## Pairs well with

- Shells: `shell-hero-stack` (single severe poster-frame), `shell-editorial-broken-grid` (asymmetric flyer-as-page), `shell-centered-column` (only when intentionally weaponised against the asymmetric default), `shell-scrapbook-substrate` (for archive / lineup montages)
- Styles: `style-oversized-neo-grotesque` (compressed display + chrome gradient), `style-brutalist-raw` (the void / hairline / mono register), `style-holographic` (the chromed wordmark itself), `style-terminal-mono` (for the info-block / manifest body register)
