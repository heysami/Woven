# Monochrome Tech Editorial (aesthetic)

**Tag:** B/W futurist editorial (NASA Apollo press kits; Kubrick *2001* stills; SpaceX mono photography; The Face tech spreads; tour-poster monochrome)

**Canonical references:**
- NASA Apollo-era press photography — rockets, suits, control rooms in silver-gelatin b/w.
- *2001: A Space Odyssey* publicity stills — hardware as monolith.
- motionsites.ai corpus: NOVA Space Systems ("ROCKETS" editorial), NeoVision (VR figure in b/w); Awwwards SOTD: Truck'N Roll (b/w fleet + condensed caps).
- Monochrome gig/tour posters — condensed caps + halftone photo.

## Cultural identity

Technology treated as ARCHIVE: black-and-white photography (rockets, machines, vehicles, figures in gear) set in a magazine-grade editorial grid with condensed grotesque caps and mono spec-text. The absence of color claims historical weight — "this hardware will be in museums." Distinct from `aesthetic-swiss-modernist` (which is typographic and abstract) because photography carries the page; distinct from terminal registers because the type is editorial, not code.

The defining gesture is the **caption discipline**: every photo gets an index number and a mono caption ("FIG. 04 — STAGE SEPARATION, 1968"), as if the page were a declassified dossier.

**Palette anchor:** paper `#f2f1ee` or ink-black `#101010` page ground, photography in true b/w (slight warm or silver tone, never blue), ink/paper inverted text, ONE optional utility accent — signal red `#e8312a` or safety orange — confined to markers and rules.

## Motifs / imagery vocabulary

- B/W hero photography: hardware, vehicles, suited figures, control rooms — grainy, documentary.
- Condensed grotesque caps at poster scale ("ROCKETS", "FULL TOURS, NO."), often cropped by the frame.
- Mono spec-text blocks: serials, coordinates, dates, payload tables.
- Index marks: FIG numbers, crosshair registration marks, file stamps.
- Halftone/grain texture on photos at print-scan fidelity.
- Grid: visible column logic, ruled hairlines, generous margins — magazine, not dashboard.

**Raster required:** the archival b/w photography (photo `orbital-space` mono-graded, `motorsport-dramatic` b/w, or archival-style generation). Color photos desaturated with CSS look flat — the grain and tonal curve must be in the asset.

## Voice register

Dossier-factual: hardware names, dates, payload numbers. Headlines are nouns ("ROCKETS", "THE FLEET"). Body reads like a flight manual with occasional swagger. Never marketing superlatives.

## Failure mode

`filter: grayscale(1)` on stock photos + Helvetica = generic mono theme. The register needs grain, captions, index marks, and condensed display caps — the ARCHIVE apparatus, not just the absence of color.

## Best for

Aerospace/defense/robotics companies, industrial heritage brands, automotive and logistics fleets, music tours and event promos, engineering portfolios, documentary microsites.
