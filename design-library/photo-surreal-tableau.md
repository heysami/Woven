# Surreal staged tableau

One impossible thing photographed with documentary realism — cloud-furniture on a lake, a doorway of light in a desert, a figure seated in a flooded field of flowers.

## Visual signatures

- EXACTLY ONE physics/scale/material violation per frame; everything else obeys reality
- Documentary light and texture — mist, haze, honest shadows — selling the impossibility
- Landscape staging: lakes at dawn, desert flats, meadows, empty interiors
- Horizon-anchored composition with generous negative sky
- Calm subjects: no action, no expression drama — dream logic with a straight face

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: surreal photographic tableau, impossible object in real landscape, scale violation, dream logic, art-directed campaign photography

**Lighting**: dawn mist or overcast softbox sky, long natural shadows, atmospheric depth haze

**Camera / lens**: medium format 50-80mm, f/11 deep focus, tripod-level horizon

**Film stock / post-processing**: muted filmic grade, lifted blacks, gentle pastel or neutral palette

**Mood**: serene, uncanny, wondrous

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: multiple impossible elements, cartoon rendering, fantasy art, HDR drama, lens flare, horror tone

## Named references

**Photographers**: Erik Johansson (seamless impossible composites), Brooke DiDonato, Hugh Kretschmer; Magritte as painting lineage

**Campaigns**: Gucci Zumi stages, Hermès window-display surrealism, Apple AirPods "Bounce" spatial logic

## Example prompt template

> A pale-blue sofa upholstered in cumulus cloud floats six inches above a glass-still mountain lake at dawn, soft mist drifting across the water and thin fog veiling the far shore. Everything else is documentary-real: honest reflections, long soft shadows, muted pastel grade with lifted blacks. No people, no other objects. Medium format, 65mm, f/11, level horizon, generous negative sky above. Serene, uncanny, wondrous — an impossible thing photographed plainly.

## When to use

Surreal-dream-stage heroes, luxury campaign microsites, fintech/insurance "impossible made safe" metaphors, editorial covers, organic-overgrowth interpenetration plates, bioluminescent deep-sea organism stages.

## When NOT to use

Trust-through-documentary briefs (news, healthcare records), price-led ecommerce, technical product detail pages.
