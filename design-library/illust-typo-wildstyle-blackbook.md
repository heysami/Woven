# Wildstyle blackbook marker

Angular interlocked wildstyle graffiti lettering worked up in marker on sketchbook paper - the writer's blackbook register, not the finished wall.

**Distinct from `illust-typo-bubble-graffiti`** - that style is rounded, inflated throw-up letterforms; this one is sharp, angular, interlocked wildstyle where letters weave through each other and sprout arrows.

## Visual signatures

- angular interlocked letterforms that weave over and under each other, hard to read on purpose
- arrows growing out of letter terminals, extending strokes into barbed points
- visible marker-stroke fills - streaky saturated color laid in diagonal passes, not flat vector fill
- heavy black outline around every letter, plus a thin colored keyline offset outside the black
- two-color gradient fills across the piece (e.g. violet blending into teal)
- loose one-line handstyle tags as the secondary voice - a single continuous marker script with halo and flick marks
- cream or off-white sketch paper ground, sometimes with faint tooth or grain
- occasional spray-mist and drip accents where the marker over-saturates

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: wildstyle graffiti, blackbook page, interlocked angular letters, arrows, marker drawing

**Material**: chisel-tip marker on cream sketch paper, streaky diagonal marker fills

**Line**: heavy black outline, thin offset colored keyline, barbed arrow terminals

**Color**: two saturated marker hues gradient-blended (violet, teal, cobalt), black outline, cream paper

**Style**: hand-drawn energy, overlapping letter weave, handstyle tag as secondary lockup, drips and spray mist accents

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: rounded bubble letters, clean vector, airbrush chrome, serif, pastel, minimal

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Phase 2, Tracy 168, Dondi White blackbook sketches, Style Wars-era NYC writers

**Movements**: 1970s-80s NYC wildstyle, blackbook sketch culture, hip-hop letterform design

## Example prompt template

> Wildstyle graffiti wordmark of [WORD] drawn in marker on cream sketchbook
> paper, angular interlocked letterforms weaving through each other with
> arrows sprouting from the terminals, streaky diagonal marker fills
> blending violet into teal, heavy black outline plus a thin offset keyline,
> a loose one-line handstyle tag below, small drips and spray-mist accents,
> blackbook sketch energy.

## When to use

Street and skate brands, music drops, battle or crew rosters, event flyers, youth culture apps that want raw writer credibility.

## When NOT to use

corporate trust, luxury restraint, quiet editorial, fintech
