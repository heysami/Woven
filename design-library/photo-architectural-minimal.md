# Architectural minimal

Concrete and glass interiors/exteriors in soft natural light, greenery as the single organic counterpoint.

## Visual signatures

- Béton-brut concrete, lime plaster, or pale stone planes filling most of the frame
- Soft directional daylight raking across surface texture — the material IS the subject
- One organic counterpoint: a fig tree, vine spill, single human figure for scale
- One-point or two-point perspective, rigorously level horizon and verticals
- Muted palette: greige, bone, shadow-gray, foliage green

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: brutalist concrete interior, raking daylight, indoor tree, architectural photography, negative space

**Lighting**: soft natural window/skylight light, long soft shadows, no artificial fixtures visible

**Camera / lens**: tilt-shift 24mm or 50mm, corrected verticals, f/8-f/11

**Film stock / post-processing**: digital clean or Portra 160 softness, low contrast, true neutrals

**Mood**: serene, monumental, contemplative

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: cluttered furnishing, HDR, saturated sky, fisheye distortion, people crowds

## Named references

**Photographers**: Hélène Binet, Iwan Baan, Julius Shulman (modernist lineage)

**Architects as subject**: Tadao Ando, Peter Zumthor, John Pawson, Ricardo Bofill (La Fábrica greenery-in-concrete)

**Magazines**: El Croquis, Dezeen features, Cereal city guides

## Example prompt template

> A tall béton-brut concrete atrium with a single mature fig tree growing from a sunken planter, photographed in soft morning skylight that rakes across the board-formed concrete texture. Corrected verticals, one-point perspective down the gallery, vast negative space above. One figure in dark clothing far down the corridor for scale. Tilt-shift 24mm, f/11, low-contrast neutral grade in the Hélène Binet tradition. Serene, monumental, contemplative.

## When to use

Real-estate and architecture portfolios, design-studio sites, premium workspace/property SaaS, restrained luxury brands, concrete-and-green tech heroes (VertexAI-style "intelligent space" briefs).

## When NOT to use

Energetic consumer products, kids/family briefs, anything needing warmth over composure.
