# Football club heraldic (aesthetic)

**Tag:** sports-club heraldry

**Canonical references:**
- Century-old European club crests (shield, ball, crown, laurel wreath) and their centenary rebrands
- Kit typography lineages - arched varsity shirt names, beveled squad numbers with contrast keylines
- Matchday programme covers and sponsor lockups ("kit + crest + wordmark" as one unit
  over a chest stripe)
- Retro kit reissues - poly-weave fabric, embroidered badges, woven collar tapes

## Cultural identity

Belonging rendered as heraldry: a deep home-navy field carrying crest-gold devices - shields, laurel wreaths, crowns, stars - with typography borrowed from the shirt itself. Names arch like a printed squad name; the number is a monument: huge, beveled, keylined in gold on navy. Everything is bordered; gold keylines frame cards, sections, and buttons the way embroidery frames a badge. Fabric is present everywhere: poly-weave texture in panels, embroidery-thread sheen in accents, a captain's-armband red used once for what matters most.

The register is tribal and ceremonial, not luxury: "FOR THE BADGE," est. dates, honors stars above the crest. It celebrates continuity - the palette is the club's forever colors, and the second palette (away ivory and stone) exists exactly the way an away kit does.

## Palette anchor

Home kit first, away kit as the inverted scheme.
- Home navy `oklch(24% 0.06 260)` - the field
- Home royal `oklch(42% 0.14 262)` - secondary panel blue
- Crest gold `oklch(75% 0.11 90)` - keylines, devices, active states
- Away ivory `oklch(95% 0.01 95)` and away stone `oklch(83% 0.01 200)` - the light scheme
- Captain red `oklch(62% 0.19 22)` - single ceremonial accent

## Decoration motifs

- The crest as master device: shield + ball + crown, flanked by laurel wreath sprigs
- Arched lettering over a monumental keylined shirt number
- Gold rule-and-title dividers (title centered in a horizontal rule, crest terminals)
- Honors stars, est. dates, and sponsor lockups as legitimate chrome
- Poly-weave and embroidery-thread raster textures inside panels and swatches
- A tricolor kit-trim stripe (navy / red / royal) as a page footer or card edge

**Raster required:** the kit itself - product shots of shirts on hanger or flat-lay, plus embroidered-crest and fabric-weave close-ups (photo register near `photo-apple-clean-studio` discipline but on navy). The heraldic devices can be SVG; the cloth cannot.

## Voice register

Ceremonial second person plural. "For the badge." "Secure your seat." "Next match." Roman-numeral and est.-date flourishes welcome; stats delivered reverently ("22/23", "VALOR 9"). Never ironic about the club, never discount-urgent.

## Failure mode

Where `aesthetic-luxury-cinematic-dark` spotlights ONE precious object in theater darkness with serif whispers, this is daylight heraldry - flat navy field, gold keylines everywhere, condensed caps, and a crowd's voice. Borrowing its cinematic falloff and italic serifs turns the club shop into a watch boutique. Opposite failure: rainbow gradients and esports-glow bevels read as a FIFA menu, not a hundred-year-old badge.

## Best for

- Club sites, matchday centers, fixtures and squad pages
- Fan membership, ticketing, and merch shops
- Fantasy leagues, supporter apps, sports media brands
- Any product selling belonging to a crest - schools, guilds, societies included
