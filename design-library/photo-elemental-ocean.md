# Elemental ocean cinematic

Open water as raw power — towering swell faces, storm texture, spray frozen mid-air — graded dark and reverent.

## Visual signatures

- The water IS the subject: wave faces, swell lines to the horizon, churned foam texture
- Dramatic marine light: storm-filtered sun, silver backlight through crests, deep slate-blue shadow
- Frozen kinetic detail — spray and droplets sharp at 1/2000s — or long-exposure silk for the calm variant
- Horizon discipline: level, placed low or high deliberately, vast sky or vast water
- No people, or one tiny vessel/swimmer for terrifying scale

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: towering ocean swell, storm sea photography, wave face detail, open water cinematic

**Lighting**: storm-filtered backlight, silver crest highlights, slate shadow depths

**Camera / lens**: water-housing 24-70mm from sea level, or 200mm compression from a vessel; 1/2000s

**Film stock / post-processing**: dark filmic grade, desaturated blue-slate, retained foam highlight detail

**Mood**: vast, reverent, elemental

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: turquoise vacation water, beach umbrellas, surf-brand saturation, HDR skies, jet-ski energy

## Named references

**Photographers**: Ray Collins (the swell-as-mountain canon), Clark Little (shorebreak), Sebastião Salgado's *Kuwait*-grade drama applied to water

**Campaigns**: Yacht Club "MASTER THE ELEMENTS"; Hashgraph's dark-ocean venture hero; watchmaker dive-line films

## Example prompt template

> A towering open-ocean swell photographed from sea level: the wave face rises like a moving cliff, storm-filtered sunlight backlighting the crest into molten silver while the trough falls into deep slate-blue shadow. Spray whips off the lip, frozen sharp at 1/2000s. Level horizon low in frame, enormous brooding sky above. Water-housing 35mm, dark filmic grade, desaturated blue-slate with crisp foam detail. No land, no people. Vast, reverent, elemental.

## When to use

Luxury-maritime briefs (yacht clubs, dive watches, sailing), elemental brand metaphors ("master the elements", "the next wave"), bioluminescent-deep surface chapters, scroll-journey descents that begin above water.

## When NOT to use

Family beach tourism, casual lifestyle, anything where the sea should feel SAFE.
