# Noir comic ink

High-contrast black-and-white ink illustration — brush splatter, dramatic spot blacks, hard-boiled narrative panels.

## Visual signatures

- Pure b/w with bold spot blacks — shadows as solid shapes, not gradients
- Expressive brush economy: dry-brush texture, ink splatter, confident gesture lines
- Noir staging: dramatic up-light, venetian-blind shadows, cigarette-smoke curls
- Comic-panel DNA: figures mid-narrative, croppings that imply a story frame
- Paper white preserved — texture lives in the ink, not the ground

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: noir ink illustration, high contrast brush drawing, comic panel figure, dramatic spot blacks, splatter accents

**Material**: sumi/india ink, dry brush, paper white ground

**Line**: bold variable-weight brush line, hatching only where halftone needed

**Color**: pure black and white; optional single red accent stamp

**Style**: hard-boiled graphic novel, editorial ink portraiture

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: grayscale gradients, color fills, manga screentone cuteness, clean vector line, soft pencil shading

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Frank Miller (*Sin City* spot blacks), Eduardo Risso, Bill Sienkiewicz (splatter expressionism), Christoph Niemann's ink editorial mode

**Movements**: noir graphic novels, editorial ink portraiture, Brand Studio / Longshot web tradition

## Example prompt template

> A hard-boiled ink illustration of a woman in a trench coat lighting a cigarette under a streetlamp, rendered in pure black india ink on paper white: solid spot-black shadows carve the figure, dry-brush texture on the coat, venetian-blind shadow stripes raking the wall behind, a few confident splatter flecks at the edges. Variable-weight brush linework, no gradients, no color. Frank Miller noir energy with editorial restraint. White background.

## When to use

Editorial features and longform storytelling, music/film promo with noir registers, brutalist or monochrome-editorial sites needing figurative art, horizontal-scroll narrative reels (the Longshot pattern).

## When NOT to use

Cheerful SaaS, healthcare reassurance, children's products, pastel registers.
