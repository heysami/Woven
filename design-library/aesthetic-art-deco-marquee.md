# Art Deco marquee (aesthetic)

**Tag:** gilded theatrical geometry

**Canonical references:**
- Radio City Music Hall / Pantages Theatre lobbies - stepped brass frames, fan grilles, lacquered ebony
- A. M. Cassandre poster lettering (1920s-30s) - inline deco display capitals
- ITC Broadway / Bifur / Parisian - the marquee letterform canon
- *The Great Gatsby* (2013) title design - the modern revival grade of the same vocabulary
- Cinema ticket and program ephemera of the premiere era - crimson, gold leaf, letterspaced caps

## Cultural identity

The opening-night register: Art Deco as pure ornament-and-lettering system, everything drawn in fine gold line on ebony lacquer. Geometry does all the glamour work - stepped ziggurat frames, sunburst fans, radiating marquee rays, chevron rules - and the lettering is the hero: inline (double-stroked) deco display capitals with high waistlines, letterspaced wide, stacked like a cinema marquee. Crimson velvet and brass are the only materials allowed to interrupt the gold-on-black line world.

Where `aesthetic-luxury-cinematic-dark` is photographic darkness - one spotlit object, macro product worship, no ornament - Art Deco marquee is DRAWN darkness: the wealth is in the linework itself, and there need not be a single photograph on the page. Every frame is an ornament opportunity; every heading gets its fan finial.

## Palette anchor

Low chroma except the crimson; the gold family carries everything.
- Ebony lacquer `oklch(16% 0.01 80)`
- Brass gold `oklch(72% 0.11 88)`
- Antique gold `oklch(62% 0.09 85)`
- Champagne pearl `oklch(88% 0.05 90)`
- Premiere crimson `oklch(45% 0.16 25)`
- Velvet maroon `oklch(34% 0.11 20)`

Never cool the black toward blue; never let the gold go neon yellow.

## Decoration motifs

- Fine gold hairline frames (1-2px) with stepped ziggurat corners; frames nest inside frames
- Sunburst / fan ornaments as section finials and dividers; radiating rays behind hero lettering
- Inline deco display capitals (double-stroke letterforms), letterspaced, stacked marquee-style
- Ticket-shaped buttons: crimson fill, gold keyline, notched deco ends
- Brushed-brass and velvet texture confined to swatch-sized panels, never full-bleed
- Chevron and stepped-line horizontal rules in place of plain borders

## Voice register

Announcer formality with showtime warmth: "NOW SHOWING", "OPENING NIGHT", "THE FEATURE HAS NOT STARTED YET." All-caps small text everywhere, letterspaced. Never slangy, never discount-urgent; the tone is an usher in white gloves.

## Failure mode

Gold gradient bevels on buttons + Trajan + drop shadows = casino, not cinema. Ornament drawn thick (4px+) collapses the fineness that reads as luxury. A blue-black background or a photographic hero turns it into `aesthetic-luxury-cinematic-dark` wearing a costume. Mixing in a geometric sans body at low letterspacing breaks the marquee spell instantly.

## Best for

- Cinema, theatre, festival and premiere microsites
- Cocktail bars, supper clubs, jazz venues, hotels with a 1920s story
- Award shows, galas, invitations and RSVP flows
- Editorial features on film history or the golden age of anything
