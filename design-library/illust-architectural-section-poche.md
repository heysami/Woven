# Architectural section poche

precise drafted building section: hard black poche fills, annotation leaders and sun-shaft overlays as ornament.

**Distinct from** `material-hand-architect-sketch`: that is loose pencil gesture; this is drafting-table precision - ruled hairlines and solid ink poche. **Distinct from** `material-cad-blueprint`: that is white CAD linework on blueprint blue; this is print-quality black-on-limewash drawing where the cut structure reads as solid black mass.

## Visual signatures

- hard black poche: every cut wall, slab and stair filled solid ink black, reading as confident mass against pale rooms
- ruled hairline interior linework: furniture, rails and floor edges drawn at one thin weight
- annotation leaders: dotted leader lines ending in small node circles, tied to inclined italic labels
- angle callouts as content: sun altitude degrees (21.2, 18.4, 7.6) stacked down the margin like a data axis
- sun-shaft overlays: translucent warm yellow beams cutting diagonally through the section as the single ornament and accent
- tiny scale figures inhabiting rooms; drafting-table register of limewash, limestone, mortar grays with one sunbeam yellow

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: architectural section drawing, poche, drafted building cut, daylight study diagram

**Material**: ink on limewash paper, drafting print, cast concrete and limestone swatch neutrals

**Line**: solid black poche fills, ruled hairlines, dotted annotation leaders with node dots, inclined italic labels

**Color**: limewash white, poche black, sunbeam yellow, shadow blue, limestone, mortar gray

**Style**: sectional cut with tiny scale figures, sun-shaft diagonal overlays, degree callouts, quiet gridded margins

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: loose sketch, blueprint blue, 3d render, cartoon, decorative ornament

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Paul Rudolph section drawings, Atelier Bow-Wow graphic anatomies

**Products / films**: competition section boards, daylight study plates

## Example prompt template

> Precise architectural section drawing for [CONCEPT], cut walls and slabs
> filled with solid black poche, ruled hairline interiors with tiny scale
> figures, dotted annotation leaders with node dots and inclined italic labels,
> sun altitude degree callouts down the margin, translucent sunbeam-yellow light
> shafts cutting diagonally through the rooms, limewash paper ground.

## When to use

Architecture and spatial products, premium editorial about place and light, dashboards that want drafted rigor, hero art for design studios.

## When NOT to use

playful kids brands, maximalist pop, grunge, cartoon registers
