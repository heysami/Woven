# Surreal Dream Stage (aesthetic)

**Tag:** Magritte-grade product surrealism (Gucci Zumi stages; Apple "Shot on iPhone" surreal campaigns; Hermès window displays; motionsites "couch-clouds in a lake" wave)

**Canonical references:**
- René Magritte — *The Empire of Light*, *Golconda*: ordinary objects, impossible placement, perfect calm.
- Hermès & Gucci campaign stagecraft — products on fur grass, in lilac voids, beside monoliths.
- motionsites.ai corpus: RIVR (cloud-couch in a misty lake), Portal (glass door in monolith desert), Asme (figure with flowers in dark field), Railroadai (train window onto another world).
- Awwwards: Gucci Zumi (red bag on lilac fur plain).

## Cultural identity

Luxury's favorite trust move: stage the product (or the idea) in a physically impossible but photographically REAL scene — a couch made of cumulus floating on a lake, a doorway of light in a desert monolith, a handbag monumental on a fur prairie. The impossibility is rendered with documentary light and texture, which is what separates it from fantasy art: it must feel like a photograph of something that cannot exist. The mood is serene, never zany — dream logic with a straight face.

The defining gesture is the **one impossible thing**: a single physics/scale/material violation per scene, everything else obeying reality strictly.

**Palette anchor:** scene-driven, but graded calm — misty neutrals `#c9cdd4`, dawn lilacs `#cbb8e8`, sand `#d9c6a5`, deep water `#26323c`; UI ink follows host style; copy panels float in the scene's quiet zone.

## Motifs / imagery vocabulary

- ONE surreal stage per page (hero) — additional sections return to disciplined product/editorial reality.
- Scale violation (monumental object in landscape), material swap (cloud-as-upholstery), or portal/window framing (door of light, train window onto elsewhere). Pick one.
- Atmospheric depth: mist, haze, long shadows — the realism budget that buys the impossibility.
- Horizon-anchored composition, generous negative sky for type.
- Serif or italic-accent headline floating in the sky zone.

**Raster required:** the entire register lives in photoreal composite imagery (photo `surreal-tableau`). There is no CSS fallback — without the photographic stage this aesthetic does not exist.

## Voice register

Dream-logic declaratives, present tense, slightly elliptical: "Assets that float." "Step through." "Where work goes quiet." Copy stays short — explanation kills a dream.

## Failure mode

Three impossible things in one frame (collage chaos, that's `avantropop`), cartoon rendering (that's fantasy illustration), lens flares and HDR drama (that's a movie poster). Also: the surreal hero followed by sections that keep being surreal — the dream must be a single held moment, then the product gets real.

## Best for

Luxury and fashion campaigns, fintech/insurance wanting "impossible made safe" metaphors, sleep/wellness brands, premium furniture and objects, brand films and launch microsites.
