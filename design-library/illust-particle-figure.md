# Particle-formed figure

A creature, object, or figure materializing from thousands of glowing particles — half-dissolved between dust and form.

## Visual signatures

- Recognizable subject (dragonfly, bird, face, hand, ring) emerging from a particle cloud
- Density gradient: tight luminous particles at the focal edge, dissolving to scattered drift
- Single hue family glow (cyan, violet, gold) against near-black
- Motion implied in the dissolve direction — particles streaming off a wingtip
- Depth via particle size falloff and faint bokeh discs

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: particle dissolve figure, glowing particle swarm forming a [subject], emergence from dust, generative particle art

**Material**: luminous points, fine bokeh, volumetric glow

**Line**: no line — density IS the contour

**Color**: single glow family (electric violet / cyan / champagne gold) on `#05060a` black

**Style**: TouchDesigner/Houdini particle render aesthetic

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: solid surfaces, rainbow multicolor, dense starfield background, cartoon outline, smoke clouds without points

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Houdini/TouchDesigner particle artists (FIELD.io tradition), Maxim Zhestkov, Quayola particle studies

**Movements**: generative design, data-as-organism visual metaphors

## Example prompt template

> A dragonfly formed from tens of thousands of glowing violet particles, wings tracing precise luminous density at their leading edges and dissolving into drifting motes behind. Particle size falls off with depth; a few soft bokeh discs float near the lens. Pure near-black background, single violet-to-magenta glow family, volumetric bloom kept subtle. Houdini-render aesthetic: emergence, intelligence, motion held mid-dissolve. Transparent or black background.

## When to use

AI/data platform heroes ("intelligence emerging"), cinematic studio brands, defi-cosmic and bioluminescent registers, dark portfolio identities. Static plate from this entry; live version is particle-gl / interactive-media territory (hand the same art direction to the drawer).

## When NOT to use

Human-warmth briefs, craft/heritage brands, light-mode pastoral registers.
