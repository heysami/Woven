# Origami engineering blueprint (aesthetic)

**Tag:** deployable-structure spec sheet

**Canonical references:**
- Koryo Miura's fold pattern and JAXA deployable solar arrays - the aerospace origami canon
- Crease-pattern diagrams (mountain / valley notation) from computational origami
- Foil-and-white satellite material palettes - gold kapton against matte structure
- Technical display lettering built from straight segments, as if scored into the sheet

## Cultural identity

An engineered sheet of paper as the whole design system: matte white stock ruled with a faint crease-pattern grid, and EVERY component - swatches, buttons, cards, nav items - drawn as a sheared parallelogram tracking one committed fold angle (about 60 degrees). The page reads as a fold in progress: display letterforms constructed from straight scored segments, mono-spaced spec codes as data ("M-128", "ANG-60", "SEQ-0042", "12,240 CREASES"), fold-diagram thumbnails inside cards, and gold foil arriving only as the precious deployable material - a clipped corner, an active fill, the logo mark.

The register is aerospace calm: the drama is latent ("EXPAND STRUCTURE IN ONE PULL"), held in precise geometry rather than motion or color. Structure IS the interface.

## Palette anchor

White sheet, pale crease lines, one metal.
- Sheet white `oklch(97% 0 0)` - the ground
- Valley blue `oklch(89% 0.03 240)` - crease gridlines and cool fills
- Mountain gray `oklch(75% 0.008 90)` - secondary structure
- Ink black `oklch(15% 0 0)` - type and rules
- Foil gold `oklch(75% 0.11 90)` - the single precious accent, used at clipped corners and active states

## Decoration motifs

- The shear: parallelogram geometry on every component, all tracking the same fold angle
- Crease-pattern gridlines (thin valley-blue diagonals over the sheet) as layout structure
- Clipped / folded corners on cards and buttons, sometimes flipped to gold as if the reverse face shows
- Mono-spaced spec-code data pairs (M-128, V-217, GRID-12, ANG-60) as textural content
- Fold-diagram line thumbnails (mountain ridges, tessellated parallelograms) inside cards
- Angle annotations (a drawn 60 degree arc) and scored-crease pressed texture

## Voice register

Mission-spec declarative. "Expand structure in one pull." "Structure becomes interface. Precision at scale, clarity in motion." Numbers carry the poetry: spans, masses, crease counts. Never whimsical-crafty, never breathless.

## Failure mode

Where `material-cad-blueprint` is cyan-line drafting on a blueprint ground - the DRAWING of a thing - this is the folded THING itself: white stock, shear geometry, gold foil; put it on cyan grid paper and you have regressed to CAD. And where `aesthetic-sculptural-minimal` softens into gallery objects and shadowless curves, this register is strictly ruled - lose the fold angle (mixed shears, random rotations) and the engineering collapses into decorative geometry. Gold spent generously reads as luxury branding, not deployable foil.

## Best for

- Aerospace, robotics, materials, and deep-tech marketing
- Products about transformation and unfolding - onboarding, expansion, scaling metaphors
- Engineering portfolios and research-lab sites
- Data-spec heavy dashboards that want elegance without color

- Shells: `shell-centered-column`, `shell-hero-stack`, `shell-bento-grid`
- Styles: `style-restrained-hairline`, `style-micro-text-frame`
- Aesthetic kin: `aesthetic-sculptural-minimal` (soft cousin), `material-cad-blueprint` (the drafting register to diverge from), `illust-origami-paper-3d` (when literal folded-paper imagery is wanted)
