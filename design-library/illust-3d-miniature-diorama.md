# 3D miniature diorama world

A tiny self-contained world - floating island, zen garden, micro-village - rendered in soft 3D with toy-scale charm.

## Visual signatures

- Self-contained scene on a floating base or circular plinth - edges visible, world-as-object
- Toy proportions: chunky trees, small houses, rounded terrain
- Soft studio lighting with gentle ambient occlusion - museum-vitrine feel
- Tilt-shift depth of field reinforcing miniature scale
- Tiny narrative details: a path, a bench, a lantern, one tiny figure

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: miniature 3D diorama, floating island, tiny world, isometric-adjacent scene, toy scale

**Material**: soft matte clay-plastic surfaces, subtle subsurface scattering on foliage

**Line**: no outlines - soft shading carries the form

**Color**: fresh pastels + natural greens, one warm accent (lantern glow, roof red)

**Style**: blender-render charm, vitrine lighting, tilt-shift miniature

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: photorealism, gritty texture, dark mood, sprawling open-world scale, hard isometric grid

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Polycount diorama school, "100 days of Blender dioramas" tradition, Animal Crossing island vignettes, Monument Valley vignettes (structural cousin)

**Movements**: cozy-game miniature aesthetics, Japanese capsule-toy dioramas

## Example prompt template

> A miniature floating island diorama: a rounded grass hill with a tiny timber cottage, two chunky low-poly pine trees, a winding pebble path to a small bench, and a paper lantern glowing warm. The island's earthen underside tapers like a pulled root ball. Soft vitrine lighting, gentle ambient occlusion, tilt-shift depth of field, fresh pastel greens with one warm accent. Blender-render charm, toy scale, cozy and self-contained. Transparent background.

## When to use

Pastoral-serene SaaS heroes wanting illustrated (not photographic) ground, onboarding/empty states as little worlds, feature sections as one diorama per capability, scroll-journey scenes traversing a chain of islands.

## When NOT to use

Enterprise gravitas, luxury registers, data-dense products where charm undermines authority.
