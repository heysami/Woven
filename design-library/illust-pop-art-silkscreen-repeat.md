# Pop art silkscreen repeat

the same portrait repeated in a serial grid, each pull drifting off register in fluoro duotones.

**Distinct from** `material-silkscreen`: that entry is only the ink-on-surface texture; this is the whole Warhol compositional system - serial repetition of one image, edition logic, deliberate off-register color shifts between pulls, and foil-and-fluoro studio glamour. **Distinct from** `material-halftone-cmyk`: that is a four-color process screen; this is hand-pulled spot color, one screen per ink, misaligned on purpose.

## Visual signatures

- one image (a face, a product, a word) repeated in rows; every repeat identical in drawing but different in registration and ink density
- coarse halftone-screened photographic source reduced to pure black ink, with fluoro pink and marigold yellow slabs floating behind it off-register
- silver foil and paper-mist grays as the ground; ink sits ON the surface, never blended into it
- factory edition furniture: edition numbers, flat price tags, deadpan grotesk labels

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: pop art silkscreen, Warhol screenprint, serial repeat, off-register, Factory edition

**Material**: silver foil ground, hand-pulled screen ink, paper fiber, foil crinkle

**Line**: coarse halftone screen from photo source, no drawn line, hard screen edges

**Color**: fluoro pink, marigold yellow, ink black duotone, silver and paper mist

**Style**: serial grid repetition of one image, deliberate registration drift, edition numbering

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: single hero image, blended gradients, painterly, muted earth tones, photoreal depth

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Andy Warhol, Corita Kent, Factory-era printers

**Products / films**: Marilyn Diptych, Campbell's Soup Cans, Flash portfolio prints

## Example prompt template

> pop art silkscreen print of [CONCEPT] repeated in a serial grid, coarse
> halftone black ink over off-register fluoro pink and marigold yellow slabs,
> each repeat drifting in registration and ink density, silver foil ground,
> hand-pulled screenprint texture, Factory edition aesthetic.
