# Premium product hero 3D (Apple / Ledger-Stax-grade)

Apple-product-marketing-grade premium device hero render - the **Apple Vision Pro / Apple Watch Ultra / Ledger Stax / Framework laptop** treatment. Sleek minimal device on a plain backdrop (gradient or pure white/black) with dramatic single key-light, accurate hi-fidelity material rendering (brushed aluminum + curved glass + leather strap + e-ink screen + chamfered edges), studio product-photography vibe. Distinct from `illust-3d-tron-emissive-glow` (which adds glow accents) - this entry is WITHOUT glow; **premium materials + dramatic lighting** do the work. The "luxury device speaks for itself" register.

## Visual signatures

- sleek minimal device - clean form, no excess detail, considered chamfers
- **accurate hi-fidelity material rendering** - brushed aluminum, curved sapphire glass, leather strap, e-ink display, anodised metal, premium polymer
- plain backdrop - pure white, pure black, OR soft gradient (white-to-light-grey, charcoal-to-black)
- **dramatic single key light** - usually from upper-front-left, soft shadow falling to lower-right
- accurate ambient occlusion at every edge / seam / chamfer (this is the AAA-grade tell)
- shot at 3/4 perspective OR straight-on hero angle
- subtle reflection of the studio backdrop on the device's glass / polished surfaces
- often a single accent - one button color, one stitching color, one screen-content moment - that anchors the eye
- the device is the WHOLE composition; no scene, no lifestyle context, no hands

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: premium product hero, Apple-grade render, sleek device, hi-fidelity materials, studio product photography

**Material**: accurate brushed aluminum + curved sapphire glass + leather strap (where applicable) + e-ink screen / OLED + chamfered metal edges, photoreal-accurate PBR materials

**Line**: no line; chamfered edges + AO are the line

**Color**: muted premium - anodised silver, midnight black, titanium grey, single accent color (one only - orange button, blue stitching, mint screen moment)

**Style**: plain backdrop (pure white / black / soft gradient), dramatic single key light, sharp soft shadow, AAA-grade ambient occlusion, 3/4 perspective or straight-on hero

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: glowing accents (use Tron-emissive entry), lifestyle context, hands, environment clutter, multiple light sources, painterly, exploded-view (use technical-exploded entry)

## Named references

**Brands**: Apple (Vision Pro, Watch Ultra, AirPods Pro, iPhone, MacBook product pages), Ledger (Stax, Flex - the hardware-wallet-as-luxury treatment), Trezor (Safe 3, Model T), Framework Laptop marketing, Nothing Phone (Carl Pei brand), Tesla Cybertruck product pages, Polestar EV reveals

**Studios**: in-house Apple Industrial Design / Apple Marcom, Layer Design, Ammunition (Beats / Polaroid OneStep), Method Studios

**Movements**: post-Jony-Ive premium-product-photography aesthetic (2010s-current), e-ink-luxury hardware wave (Ledger Stax, Daylight Computer DC1), AAA-PBR product visualization
