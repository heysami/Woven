# Pulp cover painted noir

mid-century paperback cover painting: brushed dramatic realism with tiered cover lines in taxi yellow and lipstick red.

**Distinct from** `illust-noir-comic-ink`: that style is black and white graphic ink with hard spot blacks; this is full-color painted cover realism - modelled flesh tones, wet-street neon, and a typographic cover-line system stacked on top.

## Visual signatures

- dramatic brushed realism: figures painted with confident visible strokes, theatrical studio lighting
- lurid staging: femme fatale with a revolver, backless dress, over-the-shoulder glance
- city-night subjects: neon hotel signs, wet pavement glow, silhouetted skylines in smoke blue
- tiered cover-line typography: brush-script display headline, then stacked teaser lines alternating pulp ivory, taxi yellow and lipstick red slanted italics
- accent furniture: numbered ticket tags (No 47), starburst NEW! flashes, price stamps, red slash underlines
- pulp stock ivory paper, worn leatherette grain, newsprint halftone screen in shadows

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: pulp paperback cover painting, mid-century noir, painted femme fatale, crime novel cover

**Material**: oil on board reproduction, pulp paper stock, newsprint screen, worn leatherette

**Line**: no outline, painterly brushwork, typographic tiers over art

**Color**: night blue, taxi yellow, lipstick red, pulp ivory, smoke blue, dime gray

**Style**: lurid dramatic staging, city-night neon glow, stacked slanted cover lines, ticket-number accents

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: black and white ink, flat vector, photographic, wholesome, pastel

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Robert McGinnis, Robert Maguire, Rudolph Belarski

**Products / films**: Gold Medal Books, Dell paperbacks, dime-store crime racks

## Example prompt template

> Pulp paperback cover painting for [CONCEPT], mid-century noir brushed realism,
> dramatic figure staged under neon hotel light on wet city night, tiered cover
> lines in slanted italics alternating ivory, taxi yellow and lipstick red, brush
> script display headline, numbered yellow ticket tag and NEW! flash, pulp ivory
> paper stock with newsprint screen.
