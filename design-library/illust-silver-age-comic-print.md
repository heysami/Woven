# Silver-age comic print

ben-day dot fills and misregistered CMY flats on aged pulp newsprint.

**Distinct from** `illust-spiderverse-comic-halftone`: that is a modern CG-baked comic look with painterly depth, glitch offsets, and cinematic lighting; this is the actual 1960s printing press - flat process inks, mechanical ben-day dot screens, visible registration slip, speckled pulp paper, and period hand lettering.

## Visual signatures

- flat process-color fills (process blue, action red, ben-day yellow) screened as visible dot patterns on pulp cream
- deliberate print misregistration: red and cyan plates slipping a millimeter off the black keyline
- period comic furniture: slanted shout titles with drop shadows, thought and speech balloons, starburst KRAA-POW bursts, ruled caption boxes
- confident black keyline cartooning; city skylines, square-jawed heroes, everything outlined then screened

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: silver-age comic, 1960s comic book printing, ben-day dots, vintage superhero page

**Material**: pulp newsprint, fiber speckle, process ink, registration slip

**Line**: bold black keyline, brush-inked cartooning, hand lettering

**Color**: process blue, action red, ben-day yellow and magenta dot screens, pulp cream ground

**Style**: shout title lettering, speech balloons, starburst bursts, panel caption boxes, misregistered plates

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: modern CG render, smooth gradients, painterly depth, glossy, digital glow

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Jack Kirby, Steve Ditko, Roy Lichtenstein

**Products / films**: 1963 Marvel covers, DC silver-age pages, Lichtenstein's Whaam!

## Example prompt template

> silver-age comic book art of [CONCEPT], bold black keyline cartooning with
> flat process-color fills, visible ben-day dot screens in magenta and cyan,
> deliberate print misregistration off the black plate, slanted shout title
> with drop shadow, speech balloon and starburst burst, aged pulp newsprint
> with fiber speckle, 1960s printing press aesthetic.
