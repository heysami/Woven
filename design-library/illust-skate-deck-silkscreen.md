# Skate deck silkscreen

80s-90s skate deck graphics: silkscreened screaming-skull art with brush-scrawl lettering in neon on grip-tape black.

**Distinct from** `photo-skate-zine`: that style is photographic zine documentation of skating; this is the deck artwork itself - screen-printed illustration, brush-scrawl branding and splatter ink, no photography.

## Visual signatures

- silkscreened screaming-skull art: gaping jaw, flaming hair, detailed line-shaded rendering in two or three screens
- brush-scrawl brand lettering: dry ragged strokes, all-caps, slashed underlines and circled picks in paint pen
- neon-on-black spot colors: scream aqua, hot pink, dayglo yellow and bone white punching out of grip black
- splatter ink and overspray drifting across panels, checkerboard and flame-lick borders
- deck-shop furniture: size runs (7.75 to 9.0), price scrawls, NEW DROP starburst stickers, oval brand seals
- maple veneer and sandpaper grip textures peeking behind the ink

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: skate deck graphic, 80s skateboard art, screaming skull, silkscreen street art

**Material**: silkscreen ink on maple deck, grip tape, matte splatter overspray

**Line**: brush-scrawl lettering, line-shaded skull rendering, ragged dry strokes

**Color**: scream aqua, hot pink, dayglo yellow, bone white, grip black

**Style**: screaming-skull centerpiece, checkerboard and flame borders, splatter ink, sticker seals and price scrawls

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: photographic, clean vector, pastel, corporate, soft gradients

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Jim Phillips, Vernon Courtlandt Johnson

**Products / films**: Santa Cruz Screaming Hand, Powell Peralta Ripper decks

## Example prompt template

> 80s skate deck silkscreen graphic for [CONCEPT], screaming skull with flaming
> hair rendered in line-shaded screens, brush-scrawl all-caps brand lettering
> with slashed underline, neon scream aqua, hot pink and dayglo yellow spot
> colors on grip-tape black, splatter ink overspray, checkerboard and flame-lick
> border, NEW starburst sticker.

## When to use

Streetwear and shop drops, music and event promo, youth culture campaigns, anywhere raw board-culture energy sells it.

## When NOT to use

corporate trust surfaces, healthcare, luxury restraint, quiet editorial
