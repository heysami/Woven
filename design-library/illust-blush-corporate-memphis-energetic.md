# Corporate-Memphis Energetic Action (Blush)

Bright-palette mid-action flat-vector characters - bouncy, dope, doing hobbies, group celebration. Merges Blush's Happy-Bunch / Hobbies / Shiny-Happy families. Distinct from the Scenes variant (muted, single-prop, everyday-quiet) by saturation and motion energy.

## Visual signatures

- bouncy characters mid-action - dope poses, slightly comic exaggeration
- active mid-motion (skateboarding, dancing, weight-lifting, travel, celebration)
- inclusive group compositions, holding hands, laughing
- bright friendly saturated palette
- no outline, flat fills, energetic gesture

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: flat vector, bouncy, energetic, mid-action, dope, inclusive group

**Material**: flat fill

**Line**: no line

**Color**: bright friendly saturated, sunny palette

**Style**: action pose, group composition, laughter, mid-motion

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: static, muted, dark, dramatic, realistic anatomy

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Pablo Stanley (Happy Bunch), Jal Reed (Hobbies), Brandon Mendoza (Shiny Happy)

## Example prompt template

> Bouncy flat vector character or group mid-action [skateboarding / dancing /
> celebrating / hobby], dope energetic pose, bright sunny friendly palette,
> inclusive diverse composition, no outline, comic exaggeration, Blush
> corporate-memphis energetic-action aesthetic.

## When to use

Fitness apps, hobby-marketplace SaaS, community celebration, "good vibes" brand voice, lifestyle apps for active demos.

## When NOT to use

enterprise serious, brutalism, dark themes
