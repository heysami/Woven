# Lo-fi contemplative scene

A solitary figure at rest in a vast quiet scene - dusk meadow, rooftop, window desk - rendered in the lo-fi-beats illustration tradition.

## Visual signatures

- One figure, back or profile to viewer, at rest (sitting, watching, headphones on)
- Vast quiet environment dwarfing the figure: dusk sky, meadow, city rooftop, rainy window
- Gradient hour palettes: lavender dusk, peach dawn, deep blue night with warm lamp pools
- Soft film grain and gentle bloom on lights
- Stillness with one moving element implied: drifting clouds, falling petals, steam from a mug

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: lofi illustration, solitary figure at dusk, contemplative scene, anime-adjacent calm, study-beats aesthetic

**Material**: soft digital painting, film grain, gentle bloom

**Line**: minimal clean linework on figure, painterly environment

**Color**: lavender-peach dusk gradients, warm lamp ambers against cool blues

**Style**: lo-fi hip-hop visual tradition, Shinkai-lite skies without hyperdetail

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: action poses, crowds, daylight saturation, corporate vector flatness, hyperreal detail

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Lofi Girl (ChilledCow) visual lineage, Juan Pablo Machado, "study with me" YouTube art tradition

**Movements**: lo-fi beats culture, Japanese citypop nostalgia frames

## Example prompt template

> A figure in a soft oversized sweater sits in a lavender-dusk meadow, back to the viewer, watching the last peach light fade behind distant hills. Tall grasses lean in a faint breeze; two small clouds drift. Soft digital painting with fine film grain, minimal clean linework on the figure, painterly gradient sky, one warm window glowing in a far farmhouse. Lo-fi beats album-art mood: quiet, held, gently melancholic-hopeful.

## When to use

Focus/productivity and wellbeing products ("Focus in a Distracted World"), meditation and sleep apps, personal sites with reflective registers, calm-tech heroes where photography would feel too literal.

## When NOT to use

Sales urgency, team-collaboration energy, enterprise dashboards, loud commerce.
