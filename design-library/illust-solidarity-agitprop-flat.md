# Solidarity agitprop flat

tricontinental solidarity poster: symbolic flat figures and raised fists in three to four unmodulated silkscreen inks on cream.

**Distinct from** `aesthetic-constructivism`: that register is diagonal typographic composition with photomontage; this is upright symbolic figuration - fists, flags, sunbursts - in flat poster ink. **Distinct from** `illust-saul-bass-cutout`: that has rough hand-cut scissor edges; this has clean silkscreen stencil edges with solid unmodulated ink coverage.

## Visual signatures

- symbolic flat figures: the raised fist as central emblem, crowd silhouettes with flags, marching masses reduced to one ink
- three to four unmodulated inks only: pueblo red, sun yellow, unity blue on cream paper, occasional deep maroon
- multilingual slogan banding: the same word set in Spanish, English, French and Arabic, stacked or ruled into horizontal bands
- radiating sunburst rays behind the emblem, flat stars, globes and doves as a supporting glyph set
- heavy geometric sans display type, sometimes one word per ink color
- silkscreen character: solid matte coverage, slight registration offset, paper grain showing through

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: tricontinental solidarity poster, agitprop silkscreen, raised fist emblem, protest poster

**Material**: silkscreen ink on cream paper, flat matte coverage, slight misregistration

**Line**: stencil-clean flat edges, no outline, no modeling

**Color**: pueblo red, sun yellow, unity blue, cream paper, deep maroon

**Style**: central symbolic emblem, radiating rays, multilingual slogan bands, crowd silhouettes, heavy geometric sans

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: gradients, photographic, painterly brushwork, pastel, decorative ornament

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Alfredo Rostgaard, Rene Mederos, Olivio Martinez

**Products / films**: OSPAAAL Tricontinental posters, Cuban solidarity graphics

## Example prompt template

> Tricontinental solidarity silkscreen poster for [CONCEPT], central raised
> fist emblem with radiating sunburst rays, crowd silhouettes with flags in one
> flat ink, three unmodulated silkscreen inks - pueblo red, sun yellow, unity
> blue - on cream paper, multilingual slogan bands in heavy geometric sans,
> slight registration offset and paper grain.

## When to use

Campaigns and causes, civic and community rallying surfaces, editorial about movements, bold announcement heroes.

## When NOT to use

soft lifestyle brands, luxury, whimsical kids content, neutral corporate tone
