# Dry-brush grunge display

Ragged dry-brush strokes doing the job of headline type - each letter a single fast pass of a starved brush, bristle streaks and broken edges left in. Ash white and ember orange on carbon black, with crackle and cinder texture soaking the whole frame. Fire-and-ash energy: the type looks scorched, not painted.

**Distinct from `illust-editorial-thick-brush`** - that style is clean confident thick-brush strokes with smooth edges; this one is dry, ragged and starved, edges breaking into bristle streaks and spatter. Also distinct from `material-charcoal-drawing`, which is a soft graphite surface; this is high-contrast brush calligraphy as display type.

## Visual signatures

- display letterforms drawn as single dry-brush passes - visible bristle striations inside every stroke
- ragged broken stroke edges, flicked terminals, occasional spatter at the stroke exit
- two-tone hierarchy: ember orange for the hottest word, ash white for the rest
- carbon black ground with crackle texture (fissured glaze, cracked earth) and cinder-grain speckle
- letters sit slightly off-axis, sized by force not by grid - the hand's speed is legible
- support voice in quiet monospace or grotesk caps, wide-tracked, so the brush type owns all the heat
- texture accents in the same world: crackled ash surfaces, glowing ember specks, soot gradients
- overall register is elemental process - heat, smoke, residue - rather than punk collage

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: dry-brush lettering, grunge display type, ragged brush strokes, bristle texture, scorched calligraphic headline

**Material**: starved ink brush on carbon black, crackle and cinder texture, ash speckle

**Line**: single-pass strokes, broken ragged edges, flicked terminals, spatter

**Color**: carbon black ground, ash white, ember orange accent, soot gray

**Style**: force-sized off-axis letters, quiet monospace support text, elemental heat-and-ash mood

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: smooth clean brush, rounded friendly forms, pastel colors, glossy 3D, elegant serif

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Yoji Shinkawa (brushwork energy), Niels Shoe Meulman calligraffiti, metal and streetwear brush-type lettering artists

**Movements**: calligraffiti, grunge poster typography, Japanese bokuseki expressive brush tradition

## Example prompt template

> Dry-brush display lettering of [WORD] on a carbon-black ground, each
> letter one fast pass of a starved brush with visible bristle striations,
> ragged broken edges and flicked spatter, the key word in ember orange and
> the rest in ash white, crackle and cinder texture across the surface,
> letters slightly off-axis and sized by force, a quiet wide-tracked
> grotesk caption line for contrast, scorched elemental mood.

## When to use

Music and festival drops, craft process brands (ceramics, coffee roasting, forging), sports and combat titles, dramatic campaign headlines.

## When NOT to use

friendly consumer onboarding, pastel wellness, precise fintech, kids products
