# Blueprint Hologram (aesthetic)

**Tag:** Wireframe-projection tech (Razorpay Sprint 26 wireframe shoe; Hubtown holographic cube; Iron-Man HUD lineage; CAD-render product teasers)

**Canonical references:**
- Razorpay Sprint 26 — blue wireframe 3D shoe, blueprint annotations ("SPRINT 2026").
- Hubtown — glowing holographic cube over dark real-estate UI.
- Iron Man / Minority Report HUD projections; PS5 UI accents.
- Architectural/CAD render culture: hidden-line drawings, dimension callouts.

## Cultural identity

The product as PROJECTION: wireframe meshes, hidden-line CAD drawings, and glowing holographic volumes floating in dark space, annotated with dimension lines, coordinates, and spec callouts. It says "engineered, not yet materialized" — the register of roadmaps, pre-launches, and platforms that want engineering credibility with sci-fi charisma. Distinct from `aesthetic-cyberpunk` (urban neon noir) and `aesthetic-rgb-gamer` (peripheral RGB) — this is drafting-table futurism: precise, sparse, measured.

The defining gesture is **the annotated wireframe object**: one mesh hero with dimension lines, datum points, and mono labels, glowing softly against near-black.

**Palette anchor:** drafting void `#06080f` – blueprint navy `#0a1630`, hologram cyan `#3fd2ff` / blueprint blue `#3f6fff`, grid lines at 8-15% opacity, ink `#e8f1f8`, ONE warm accent (amber `#ffb547`) for highlights/CTAs.

## Motifs / imagery vocabulary

- ONE wireframe/holographic hero object: product mesh, building volume, vehicle, chip — edges glowing, faces transparent or scanline-filled.
- Annotation apparatus: dimension lines with arrowheads, datum circles, coordinate readouts, `R 42.0` callouts in mono.
- Faint blueprint grid or graph-paper ground; section-cut hatching as texture.
- Scan effects: a sweep line materializing the mesh, vertices flickering in (once, on entry — not looping).
- UI chrome stays flat and dark; the hologram is the only volumetric event.

**Raster/3D required:** the wireframe hero (illust `wireframe-3d`, or live three.js edges-material — sim/im territory for interactive variants). CSS gradients can't draw a mesh.

## Voice register

Spec-sheet confidence: measurements, version numbers, codenames. "SPRINT 26 — 240g. 4mm drop. Shipping Q3." Roadmap future-tense permitted. Never mystical, never marketing-fluffy.

## Failure mode

Random glowing grid + lens flares = sci-fi wallpaper. The register lives in the ANNOTATIONS — no dimension lines, no datum marks = just a blue mesh. Also: three holograms per viewport (one projection per stage), and rainbow holographic gradients (that's `style-holographic`, a different material).

## Best for

Product pre-launches and roadmaps, hardware/robotics/chip companies, real-estate development ("we build the future"), sports-tech drops, engineering platforms, fintech infrastructure.
