# Eco-infrastructure heroic

Clean-energy hardware in living landscapes — wind turbines over meadows, solar fields at golden hour, terraced farms from the air.

## Visual signatures

- Renewable hardware (turbines, solar arrays, greenhouses) integrated INTO thriving landscape, not industrial wasteland
- Golden-hour or soft overcast light; atmosphere haze for depth
- Aerial or elevated vantage showing pattern: turbine rows, panel grids, terrace contours
- Foreground biology: wildflowers, crops, grasses sharp against distant hardware
- Honest scale — human figures or vehicles as occasional measure

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: wind turbines over flowering meadow, solar farm at golden hour, aerial terraced fields, clean energy landscape

**Lighting**: golden hour side-light or bright soft overcast, atmospheric haze layers

**Camera / lens**: drone aerial 24mm, or 70-200mm compressed landscape from a ridge

**Film stock / post-processing**: digital clean, gentle warm grade, true greens (no teal-crush)

**Mood**: hopeful, productive, calm abundance

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: smokestacks, dystopia, oversaturated HDR, gray industrial sky, clipart sun flare

## Named references

**Photographers**: Edward Burtynsky (pattern vantage, optimistic inversion), George Steinmetz (agricultural aerials)

**Campaigns**: Ørsted and Vestas brand films, Patagonia provisions landscapes, Apple 2030 carbon-neutral spots

## Example prompt template

> Aerial view at golden hour: rows of white wind turbines striding across rolling meadow hills, wildflower bloom sharp in the foreground slope, layers of atmospheric haze separating each ridge into the distance. A single service truck on a dirt track gives scale. Drone photograph at 24mm, gentle warm grade with honest greens, soft long shadows. Hopeful, productive, calm abundance — clean-energy landscape photography.

## When to use

Climate/energy platforms, agritech, sustainability dashboards, ESG reporting sites, pastoral-serene SaaS heroes needing a real-world ground, solarpunk briefs needing photographic (not illustrated) treatment.

## When NOT to use

Product-macro briefs, interior/architectural subjects, dark-register pages.
