# Night-market brush vernacular (aesthetic)

**Tag:** stall-sign lantern warmth

**Canonical references:**
- East Asian night-market stall signage - Raohe and Shilin (Taipei), yatai rows and matsuri food stalls (Japan)
- Hand-painted awning banners and cloth flags: rough brush display in saffron, white, and red on deep ground
- Kraft-card price signs with fat marker numerals (燒餃子 $80) clipped to the stall front
- Woodcut-stamped shop marks - the fish, dumpling, and lantern blocks printed on menus and bags

## Cultural identity

The graphic language of a night market at full steam: rough brush display lettering - saffron, white, and red - on an ink-navy night ground, strings of paper lanterns supplying the light and the palette, and every price on its own kraft card written in confident marker with the $-figure huge. Steam curls through the lantern glow; woodcut stamps (a fish for the seafood stall, a red seal for the popular one) badge the signs; awning canvas and wet asphalt carry the texture. Composition is a happy jumble with a system underneath: the brush does display, the marker does prices, the stamps do identity, and warmth does everything else.

Differentiation: `illust-ink-wash-atmosphere` uses the same brush tradition for contemplative monochrome - misty mountains, meditative emptiness; here the brush is COMMERCIAL and loud, selling dumplings at volume in three colors. And against `aesthetic-tokyo-signboard-canyon` (fabricated electric signage - neon tubes, backlit acrylic, channel letters made by sign shops), the night market is HANDMADE: brush on cloth, marker on kraft, woodcut on paper - nothing plugged in but the lantern string.

## Palette anchor

Lantern light against ink-navy night:
- Lantern saffron `oklch(80% 0.15 70)`
- Lantern gold `oklch(85% 0.14 90)`
- Banner red `oklch(50% 0.18 27)`
- Awning blue `oklch(38% 0.10 262)`
- Steam white `oklch(95% 0.01 90)`
- Ink-navy asphalt `oklch(15% 0.03 260)`
- Kraft card `oklch(70% 0.05 75)`

Saffron and gold carry the glow; red is the seal-and-banner accent; the navy ground stays deep, never gray.

## Decoration motifs

- Rough brush display lettering with visible dry edges, saffron or white on navy
- Kraft price cards with fat marker CJK item names and oversized $ numerals
- Lantern strings as literal light source and as garland ornament
- Woodcut stamp marks: fish, dumpling, and seal blocks in one or two inks
- Awning-canvas texture panels and cloth banner flags with notched tails
- Steam wisps drifting through compositions; red 人氣 (popular) tab seals on best-sellers

**Raster required:** the brush display lettering, woodcut stamps, and kraft-card textures (typography `market-brush-lockup`, texture `kraft-marker-card`, illustration `woodcut-stall-stamp`). Marker pressure and brush drag do not survive as fonts.

## Voice register

Stall-keeper direct: item, method, price - 烤鯖魚, 炭火現烤, $120. Superlatives are earned tags (每日現包 - made fresh daily), not adjectives. Short, appetizing, numeric. Never menu-formal, never lifestyle-brand airy - if a line could not be shouted over a grill, cut it.

## Failure mode

Typeset "brush fonts" and printed-looking price tags gut it - the marker and brush must read as written tonight, by hand, by someone busy. Second tell: cooling the palette or graying the navy toward slate; the register lives on lantern warmth against deep night. Third tell: tidy grid alignment of the cards - stall signs hang, lean, and overlap; perfect rows read as a supermarket. Fourth tell: adding neon or backlit signage, which walks it into the signboard-canyon register and loses the handmade contract.

## Best for

- Food discovery apps, street-food brands, restaurant menus with market roots
- Festival and event pages, pop-up and vendor marketplaces
- Grocery/delivery features for fresh and daily-made goods
- Any commerce brief wanting appetite plus handmade trust
