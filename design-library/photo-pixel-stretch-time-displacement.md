# Pixel-stretch / time-displacement photo effect

One row or column of pixels — typically selected from the subject's eye-line, lip-line, or strongest contour — **stretched horizontally or vertically across the entire frame**, creating a surreal smeared-motion streak. The classic Photoshop pixel-stretch technique. Used for album art (Coldplay, LCD Soundsystem-era covers), music videos, slow-burn editorial, abstract magazine spreads.

## Visual signatures

- a single horizontal (or vertical) row of pixels stretched across the frame
- the stretched zone reads as motion-blur even though nothing was actually moving
- subject's recognisable form sits in some portion of the frame (top-third or bottom-third), with the stretch filling the rest
- color information of the stretched row dominates — often a single warm or single cool band
- distinct from datamosh / RGB-shift in that the rest of the frame is CLEAN — the stretch is a single deliberate gesture, not chaos
- may be circular pixel-stretch (radial from a centre point) instead of linear

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: pixel stretch, time displacement, single row stretched, smeared motion, Photoshop pixel-stretch

**Lighting**: original subject lighting any; the stretch effect is post-processed

**Camera / lens**: any source — the effect is post-production

**Film stock / post-processing**: pixel-row selection from a strong contour, stretched horizontally OR vertically across the entire frame, optional circular radial variant

**Mood**: surreal, abstract, slow-time, contemplative, music-video-still

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: chaotic, multi-channel-glitch, datamosh, RGB-split — pixel-stretch is a SINGLE deliberate gesture, not glitch chaos

## Named references

**Editorial / music examples**: Coldplay "Mylo Xyloto" era covers, Caribou album art, abstract magazine spreads in The Gentlewoman / Apartamento conceptual sections, Pitchfork album-review hero images

**Movements**: post-2010 abstract album-art, slow-burn editorial photo, conceptual photography

## Example prompt template

> Editorial photograph of [SUBJECT — portrait / landscape / still life]
> with a single horizontal row of pixels from the subject's eye-line
> **stretched vertically across the entire upper-half of the frame**,
> creating a surreal smeared motion-streak even though nothing was actually
> moving. The lower-half of the frame keeps the subject clean and
> recognisable. The stretched zone's color band dominates the upper half —
> warm-amber if portrait, or matching the strongest contour color. Surreal,
> slow-time, contemplative mood. Distinct deliberate gesture (not chaotic
> glitch). Photoshop pixel-stretch / time-displacement editorial aesthetic.
