# Bollywood painted poster

heroic hand-painted film portraiture with chunky two-script showcard titles on aged poster paper.

**Distinct from** `illust-classical-painting-remix`: that style collages existing museum oil paintings into modern layouts; this is original showcard cinema painting - brush-modelled faces, montage staging, and bevel-extruded billing-block lettering painted as one piece.

## Visual signatures

- heroic oil/gouache painted portraiture, visible dry-brush strokes in skin and hair
- dramatic warm lighting: fire orange and film gold blazing against deep indigo shadow
- montage composition: one oversized hero head, smaller supporting portraits, action vignettes (trucks, running figures, burning skyline) stacked below
- chunky bevel-extruded showcard title lettering in two scripts (Latin plus Devanagari), gold face with red keyline and hard drop shade, chrome sparkle highlights
- aged poster paper: cream stock, wall grit, sun-faded edges, hand-ruled border frames
- star bursts and small ornament dividers between billing lines

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: hand-painted Bollywood film poster, 1970s Hindi cinema, heroic painted portrait, montage poster

**Material**: oil and gouache on poster paper, dry-brush texture, aged paper grain, wall grit

**Line**: no outline, brush-modelled form, painted edges

**Color**: crimson, film gold, light ochre, indigo shadow, poster cream, blood maroon

**Style**: dramatic warm rim light, stacked hero montage, bevel-extruded two-script showcard title

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: photoreal, clean vector, soft pastel, digital gradient, flat design

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Diwakar Karkare, C. Mohan, the Bombay poster ateliers

**Products / films**: Sholay, Deewaar, Amar Akbar Anthony one-sheets
