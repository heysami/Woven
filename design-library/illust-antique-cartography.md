# Antique nautical cartography

a full chart drawing system: rhumb lines, hatched coasts, compass roses, sepia ink on stained vellum.

**Distinct from** `material-parchment`: that entry is only the substrate surface; this is the complete cartographic drawing language on top of it - the coastline hatching, the sounding numerals, the rose geometry, the cartouche calligraphy, the plotted route.

## Visual signatures

- coastlines drawn with short perpendicular hatch strokes fading seaward; interiors left pale, water filled with depth-sounding numerals and contour dashes
- navigation geometry as ornament: compass roses, radiating rhumb lines, glyph legends, coordinate margins
- sepia iron-gall ink with feathered edges and granular settling; a single fading-red accent for the plotted route or the X
- salt-stained tan vellum with creases, tide blooms, and worn torn corners; calligraphic charter-hand titles in a ruled cartouche

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: antique nautical chart, treasure map, cartography, hand-drawn coastline, 17th century navigation

**Material**: salt-stiffened vellum, iron-gall sepia ink, tide stain, worn creases

**Line**: coastal hatch strokes, dashed depth contours, ruled rhumb lines, calligraphic charter hand

**Color**: vellum tan, ink sepia, deep ink black, single fading-red route accent

**Style**: compass rose, sounding numerals, cartouche title, dashed route to an X

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: satellite realism, flat modern map UI, neon, clean vector, gradients

## Named references

**Illustrators**: Gerardus Mercator, Willem Blaeu, Golden Age Dutch chartmakers

**Products / films**: Mercator's Atlas, Blaeu sea charts, Treasure Island endpaper maps
