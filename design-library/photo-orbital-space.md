# Orbital space photoreal

Planet limb with thin atmosphere rim-glow against true-black space, photographed from orbit.

## Visual signatures

- Planet limb (horizon arc) cropped at frame edge, never a full centered globe
- Thin atmosphere rim-glow - electric blue scattering band tracing the curve
- Satellite / spacecraft hardware lit by planetshine: foil, panels, antennae in crisp detail
- Night-side city lights, cloud systems, aurora curtains as surface texture
- Sparse real-exposure stars; sun as a hard point source with honest lens bloom

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: Earth limb from low orbit, atmosphere rim glow, satellite in foreground, planetshine lighting, orbital photography

**Lighting**: single hard sunlight, planetshine fill, true-black shadow side, thin blue scattering band at horizon

**Camera / lens**: ISS cupola photography, 24-50mm, deep depth of field

**Film stock / post-processing**: digital clean, slight teal-orange grade on surface, no HDR halos

**Mood**: vast, operational, calm competence

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: centered cartoon globe, dense starfield wallpaper, purple nebula, lens flare streaks, sci-fi fantasy planets

## Named references

**Photographers**: NASA Johnson archive, Thomas Pesquet ISS photography

**Campaigns**: SpaceX webcast frames, Starlink coverage imagery, Planet Labs marketing

**Films**: *Gravity* opening shot, *Interstellar* docking exteriors

## Example prompt template

> View from low Earth orbit: the planet's limb sweeps across the lower third of the frame, a thin electric-blue atmosphere rim glowing against true-black space. A communications satellite with gold foil insulation and extended solar panels floats in the upper right, lit half by hard sunlight and half by soft blue planetshine. Below, night-side city lights trace coastlines under scattered moonlit clouds. Sparse faint stars, honest exposure. Shot like ISS cupola photography, 35mm, deep focus, digital-clean with a subtle teal grade. Vast, operational, calm.

## When to use

Cosmic-horizon SaaS heroes, satellite/geo/earth-observation products, global-infrastructure positioning, climate platforms, "planet-scale" AI marketing. Mono-grade it for monochrome-tech-editorial briefs.

## When NOT to use

Human-warmth briefs (HR, community, food), small-business tools, anything where vastness reads as distance from the customer.
