# Sodium monochrome installation (one-hue world)

Photography inside a monofrequency-light environment - a vast interior flooded by a single sodium-amber source, so the ENTIRE frame collapses into one hue. A huge glowing disc or light field hangs in haze; people become flat black silhouettes lying, standing and drifting across the floor. Every surface, skin tone and garment reads only as amber-vs-black. The picture is effectively a duotone made by physics, not by grading.

**Distinct from `photo-golden-hour-indoor-warm`** - that style is warm but FULL-COLOR domestic sunlight with props, skin tones and interiors intact; this one is a monofrequency installation where color is physically deleted and figures become anonymous silhouettes in a monumental space.

## Visual signatures

- one sodium-amber hue owns the whole frame - highlights, midtones and haze are all the same color family
- figures rendered as pure black silhouettes, no readable faces or garment color - bodies become markers of scale
- a dominant glowing source in frame: a giant sun-disc, a mirrored ceiling doubling the light, a luminous horizon band
- artificial mist / haze fills the volume so light becomes a visible atmosphere, softening distance into amber fog
- monumental architectural scale - tiny people under a vast luminous void
- flat tonal compression: the image reads as amber-and-black duotone with a soft glow bloom around the source
- figures often prone or scattered on the floor, casual and ritual at once
- compositions are frontal and symmetrical, or high wide views that map the crowd as dark punctuation

## Prompt keywords

**Primary**: monofrequency sodium light installation, one-hue amber world, silhouetted figures in haze, giant indoor sun, monumental light environment

**Lighting**: single sodium-amber monofrequency source flooding the space, visible haze and glow bloom, mirrored ceiling or luminous disc as the source, no secondary color temperature anywhere, deep black shadow silhouettes

**Camera / lens**: full-frame mirrorless with 24-35mm wide prime for the architectural scale, stopped to f/5.6-f/8, tripod, low ISO; long views that keep tiny figures sharp

**Film stock / post-processing**: digital capture graded to a strict amber/black duotone - crush every other hue, keep glow bloom around the source, gentle lift in the haze, no HDR, fine grain only

**Mood**: awe, hush, ritual gathering, sublime artificial weather, anonymous collective presence

**Avoid (negative prompt)**: full-color scene, readable faces, mixed light temperatures, domestic interior props, blue sky, studio product light, saturated multicolor

## Named references

**Photographers / artists**: Olafur Eliasson "The Weather Project" documentation (Tate Turbine Hall), James Turrell installation photography, monofrequency room works, Anthony McCall light-solid documentation

**Movements**: large-scale light installation art, sublime-atmosphere museum photography

## Example prompt template

> Wide interior photograph of a vast hall flooded by a single
> sodium-amber monofrequency light, a giant glowing sun-disc hanging in
> artificial mist, the entire frame collapsed into one amber hue with soft
> glow bloom, dozens of people reduced to flat black silhouettes lying and
> standing on the floor as tiny scale markers, mirrored ceiling doubling
> the light, strict amber-and-black duotone, monumental symmetry, hushed
> sublime mood, fine grain, no other color anywhere in frame.

## When to use

Museum and exhibition promotion, ambient music and event visuals, meditation or light-therapy products, monumental brand moments, editorial features on atmosphere and perception.

## When NOT to use

e-comm product clarity, friendly consumer lifestyle, food, corporate headshots, colorful youth branding
