# DePIN hardware (aesthetic)

**Tag:** `aesthetic-depin-hardware`

## Cultural identity

DePIN = Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks — a 2022-onward category where crypto-token incentives bootstrap physical infrastructure (wireless networks, EV chargers, GPU mesh, mapping cars, weather stations, energy grids). The marketing aesthetic combines hardware-product photography with crypto-native tokenization language. The audience is half-startup-buyer half-on-chain-investor, so the brand must convey both "this is real machinery" and "this is yield-bearing infrastructure". Distinct from generic dark tech marketing by its specific dual identity: physical asset + on-chain incentive layer.

## Palette anchor

Dark tech surface as default — `#0D0F14` near-black with warm undertone or `#0A1018` cool deep-blue. Single bright neon accent for token-yield indicators — typically electric cyan `#00D9FF`, lime `#84FF00`, or magenta `#FF2D5F`. Hardware product renders almost always against the dark backdrop with rim lighting in the brand accent. White text on dark with one warm-grey tier `#8A8F9A` for secondary readouts. Generally desaturated except for the neon accent and the brand-tinted hardware rim light.

## Decoration motifs

- 3D-rendered hardware hero (Cinema 4D / Blender / Spline / KeyShot)
- Architectural product photography of physical hardware with neon-accent rim lighting
- Network map graphics showing global deployment of nodes
- Token-yield calculator embedded inline ("earn $X / month per node")
- Geographic coverage maps with live node markers
- Hardware spec callouts in mono ("4G LTE", "100W bidirectional", "16TH/s")
- On-chain proof links (transaction hashes, contract addresses) as social proof
- Live network metrics (active nodes, daily uptime, tokens distributed)
- Cyberpunk-adjacent neon highlight strips on hardware edges

## Voice register

Half-startup-pitch half-on-chain-protocol. "Deploy a node. Earn protocol rewards." / "Bandwidth as a tradeable asset". Hardware specs in mono. Token economics explained as yield curves. Direct address to both operator ("you" running a node) and investor ("the network" earning fees). No marketing exclamation, no degenerate-crypto slang — this aesthetic is more straight-faced than `crypto-degen` because real hardware costs money and operators need confidence.

## Failure mode

Looking like generic IoT marketing (think Cisco / Bosch / Siemens) kills the aesthetic — the on-chain incentive layer must be visible in the copy and the live data. Other failures: removing the dark backdrop (this aesthetic dies in light mode), generic stock photography of hardware (commissioned 3D renders or branded product shots are mandatory), pure crypto-trading-app voice (this is hardware, not speculation), missing the geographic / network coverage element (the "decentralized" claim needs visible proof).

## Best for

Decentralized wireless networks (LoRa, 4G, 5G), distributed GPU compute marketing, EV-charger networks with token incentives, mapping / sensor / weather networks, energy-grid token incentives, distributed storage hardware, anywhere physical infrastructure is marketed simultaneously to operators-as-buyers and token-holders-as-investors. NOT for: pure-software crypto products (use `crypto-degen` or `defi-cosmic`), enterprise hardware without token layer (use `bloomberg-dashboard` or `ai-foundry-dark`).

## Pairs well with

- Shells: `hero-stack` (default — marketing for the network), `bento-grid` (for product-feature spread), `canvas-floating` (for the network-operator dashboard variant)
- Styles: `dense-mono-dark`, `aurorism` (for the soft-tech variant), `bold-display` (for the product-launch variant)
