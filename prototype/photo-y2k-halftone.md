# Y2K halftone newsprint effect (photo)


**Tag:** photo-y2k-halftone
  ·  **Era:** 2000s revival
  ·  **Category:** archival

Photo passed through visible CMYK halftone dot pattern.

**Visual signatures**
- Photo passed through visible CMYK halftone dot pattern
- Slight registration misalignment between channels
- Newsprint paper warmth, slight yellowing
- Saturated electric pink and cyan

**When to use**

Music posters, zine, streetwear lookbook, Gen-Z marketing, hero images for editorial websites that want to reference print culture.
