# Keith Haring radiant pop (illust)


**Tag:** illust-keith-haring-radiant
  ·  **Category:** Mid-century / vintage

thick uniform black lines defining all forms.

**Visual signatures**
- thick uniform black lines defining all forms
- simple cartoon figures, often dancing
- radiant motion lines around figures
- block-color background, no shading

**When to use**

Pride campaigns, activism brands, youth culture, music venues.

**Pairs with** (prototype slugs)

- `aesthetic-positivity-kawaii`
- `aesthetic-maximalism`
- `aesthetic-anti-design`
