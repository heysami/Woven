# Hands Up (Eurodance vector) (aesthetic)

**Tag:** aesthetic-vector-hands-up

**Canonical references:**
- Cascada *Everytime We Touch* 2006 — chrome-italic wordmark + magenta/night-blue sleeve, the genre's defining cover
- Future Trance Vol. 38-50 (2006-2010) — overhead lens-flare sunburst, raised-arms crowd silhouette, German compilation grammar at full strength
- Dream Dance Vol. 42-49 — cyan godray + four-point star twinkles as recurring visual signature
- Groove Coverage *21st Century* — flat hard-edge vector silhouette over gradient sky, no horizontal grid
- Manian / Rob Mayth label sleeves 2005-2009 — track-listing density married to extended-italic display type

## Cultural identity

Hands Up is the visual language of mid-to-late-2000s European hard-dance and Eurodance compilation culture — Cologne, Frankfurt, Rotterdam superclubs and the German "Vol. 47" mix-CD industrial complex. It is NOT synthwave, NOT vaporwave, NOT Outrun. The era is 2005-2010, not 1985 nostalgia. The mood is overhead arena lights at 3am, hands literally up, magenta-into-night-blue sky, one laser, one chrome word. It signals euphoric club energy, mass-market dance pop, and a specific German graphic-design pragmatism — extended italic display type doing all the work over a cheap-but-precise gradient.

The aesthetic is RESTRAINED maximalism: pick ONE silhouette, ONE lens flare, ONE chrome wordmark, and let the gradient and magenta glow do the heavy lifting. Crowded "rave salad" compositions break the spell.

## Palette anchor

- Magenta `#E6007E` — the genre signature, used for separators, glows, rim-lights
- Hot cyan `#00AEEF` (laser `#00E5FF`) — godray and accent ray, never as fill
- Deep night `#0A0E2C` — top of sky gradient
- Mid royal-violet `#5A1A6E` / `#1B1561` — gradient mid-tones
- White `#FFFFFF` — silhouette fill, star twinkles, chrome specular

The palette is COOL. No warm greys, no sepia, no peach, no sunset-orange. If grey is needed, use cool steel `#C8D2E0` only.

## Decoration motifs

MANDATORY vocabulary:
- One overhead lens-flare sunburst at top-center of the composition
- One flat-fill vector silhouette with raised arms (single dancer or compressed crowd row)
- One chrome-beveled italic-extended ALL-CAPS wordmark
- 3-5 four-point vector "star" twinkles scattered near the flare
- One cyan laser ray cutting through the sky

FORBIDDEN vocabulary:
- Palm trees, dolphins, Greek busts (vaporwave)
- Horizontal sunset grid / Outrun road (synthwave)
- RGB gradient mesh, soft pastel washes (aurorism)
- Rounded humanist sans (Inter / Geist / Helvetica)
- Sepia, peach, warm pinks
- Photographic bokeh — twinkles are vector star primitives, not blurred dots
- Lowercase friendly wordmarks

## Voice register

All-caps German-compilation-DJ grammar. "VOL. 47", "THE BEST OF HANDS UP", "MIXED BY", "NON-STOP DJ MIX". Track titles in ALL-CAPS title-case. Numbers are loud and proud (volume numbers, BPM, year). Never lowercase, never humanist, never friendly. Subtitles are tracked +120. Microcopy is dense (compilation track listings are crowded by design).

## Failure mode

The "Y2K rave salad" tell: stacking a synthwave horizontal grid + a palm-tree silhouette + Inter sans + pastel pink + a soft-blur drop shadow + a Greek bust. Result reads as Outrun / Vaporwave cosplay, never as Hands Up. Hands Up is post-2005, COOL-palette, hard-edge vector, extended-italic. Other tells: rounded pill buttons, warm greys, lowercase wordmarks, photographic lens-flare PNGs instead of clean vector starbursts.

## Best for

- Dance / music compilation covers and landing pages
- Club-night flyers and event hubs
- Festival lineup pages with euphoric arena energy
- Gym / cardio / spin-class playlists
- Anything that wants 2007-Cologne-superclub euphoria without modern minimalism

Not for: finance, wellness, productivity dashboards, content-dense reading, anything that needs a calm or trustworthy register.
