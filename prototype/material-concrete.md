# Concrete (raw industrial) (material)


**Tag:** material-concrete
  ·  **Family:** analog  ·  **Category:** stone  ·  **Surface:** matte  ·  **Transparency:** opaque

A matte surface that reacts to light: no specular.

**Examples in the wild**

- Bauhaus poster substrates
- architectural photo overlays

**Common implementation mistakes (avoid these)**

- too clean (concrete is messy)
- no cracks or stains
- flat colour with no aggregate visible
