# Frontline war photojournalism (photo)


**Tag:** photo-war-photojournalism
  ·  **Era:** 1990s-current
  ·  **Category:** documentary

35mm or 28mm wide lens, very close to action.

**Visual signatures**
- 35mm or 28mm wide lens, very close to action
- Available light only, often harsh midday or smoke-filtered
- Saturated color (Kodachrome era) or stark mono
- Subject mid-trauma or mid-relief, unstaged

**When to use**

Editorial only — news, documentary film stills, NGO campaign. NEVER for consumer brand work.

**Pairs with** (prototype slugs)

- `recipe-newspaper-of-record`
- `style-agate-broadsheet`
