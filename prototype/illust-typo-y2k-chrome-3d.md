# Y2K Chrome 3D typography (illust)


**Tag:** illust-typo-y2k-chrome-3d
  ·  **Category:** Illustrative typography

bulbous 3D letterforms with chrome / liquid metal surface.

**Visual signatures**
- bulbous 3D letterforms with chrome / liquid metal surface
- environment-mapped reflections (sky, neon, gradient)
- drop shadow with cyan or pink bleed
- paired with neon backgrounds, lens flares, sparkles

**When to use**

Y2K revival branding, music marketing, beauty drops, Gen-Z fashion.

**Pairs with** (prototype slugs)

- `aesthetic-y2k-futurism`
- `aesthetic-y2k-memphis-loud`
- `aesthetic-vaporwave`
- `aesthetic-frutiger-chromecore`
