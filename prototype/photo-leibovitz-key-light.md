# Annie Leibovitz one-light portraiture (photo)


**Tag:** photo-leibovitz-key-light
  ·  **Era:** 1980s-current
  ·  **Category:** editorial-fashion

Single large softbox or umbrella, ambient kept in.

**Visual signatures**
- Single large softbox or umbrella, ambient kept in
- Subject in their world, with their objects, doing their thing
- Painterly chiaroscuro, deep shadows but not crushed
- Conceptual: subject costumed as themselves or a character

**When to use**

Founder portraits, About Us photography, magazine-style profile shots, conference speaker headshots that need to feel cinematic. The default for "tell their story" briefs.
