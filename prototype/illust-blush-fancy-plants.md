# Fancy Plants (illust)


**Tag:** illust-blush-fancy-plants
  ·  **Category:** Hand-drawn / sketch

hand-painted plants in terracotta or ceramic pots.

**Visual signatures**
- hand-painted plants in terracotta or ceramic pots
- gouache-like matte texture with visible brush
- soft earth-tone palette — sage, terracotta, cream
- no characters, just botanical objects

**When to use**

Cottagecore, wellness brands, plant care, food editorial, cozy

**Pairs with** (prototype slugs)

- `aesthetic-cottagecore`
- `aesthetic-solarpunk`
- `aesthetic-coastal-grandmother`
- `recipe-warm-restraint`
