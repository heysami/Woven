# Felt (matted wool, fuzzy) (material)


**Tag:** material-felt
  ·  **Family:** analog  ·  **Category:** fabric  ·  **Surface:** matte  ·  **Transparency:** opaque

A matte surface that reacts to light: no specular and deforms: yes — squashes on press.

**Common implementation mistakes (avoid these)**

- smooth colour with no fuzz
- no soft edges (felt cuts soft)

**Pairs with** (prototype slugs)

- `style-skeuomorphism (poker felt`
- `billiards)`
- `aesthetic-dark-academia`
- `aesthetic-cottagegoth`
