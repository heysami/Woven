# Blackletter / Neo-Gothic illustrative (illust)


**Tag:** illust-typo-blackletter-neo-gothic
  ·  **Category:** Illustrative typography

blackletter foundation with sharp ornate flourishes.

**Visual signatures**
- blackletter foundation with sharp ornate flourishes
- high stroke contrast, broken-pen angularity
- paired with cross, dagger, flame ornament
- mono black or single saturated color

**When to use**

Streetwear, metal music, dark editorial, gothic brands.

**Pairs with** (prototype slugs)

- `aesthetic-cottagegoth`
- `aesthetic-dark-academia`
- `aesthetic-corporate-grunge`
