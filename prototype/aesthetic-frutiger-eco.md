# Frutiger Eco (aesthetic)

**Tag:** `aesthetic`

**Canonical references:**
- Method cleaning bottles 2006–2012 — the dew-on-glass-bottle plate that defined the era
- Wall-E 2008 — single seedling-in-boot as the hopeful Eco motif
- Innocent Smoothies mid-2000s packaging — warm-stewardship voice register
- Plants vs. Zombies 2009 — glossy-green CTAs over sunlit grass
- BP Helios / "Beyond Petroleum" 2000–2010 — corporate-greenwash gloss of the moment

## Cultural identity

The "green" sub-dialect of Frutiger Aero, peaking 2006–2012 when sustainability marketing went mainstream and every brand wanted dew-beaded leaves over glass chrome. Where parent Aero promised tech-utopia (dolphins, sky, gadgets), Eco promised earnest stewardship of nature — the same translucent glass-on-photographic-plate scaffold but with macro foliage replacing sky-and-water.

The era is post-Inconvenient-Truth optimism: climate concern as a brand-building opportunity, not yet a crisis. The voice is warm-corporate-hopeful, the imagery is dewy and abundant, the future is solvable.

## Palette anchor

Green leads but the sky-blue half of the parent Aero palette is ALWAYS retained — pure-green-only reads as Whole Foods signage, not Eco.

- Leaf-green primary `#5EC343`, deep moss `#3E8B2A`, grass `#71AB23`
- Sky-cyan `#66BECF`, sky `#BFE6F5` (the retained Aero blue)
- Raindrop blue `#1E6CDC` for secondary CTAs
- Sun-yellow `#F4C842` or honey-amber `#DEAA41` as a single solar/dewdrop highlight
- Greys are warm-green-tinted, never neutral; body ink is warm dark-olive, never `#000`

## Decoration motifs

Macro-photography or skeuomorphic 3D render only — flat vector leaves are forbidden. Pick ONE motif per page and commit:

- A single dew-beaded leaf
- ONE seedling pushing through soil
- Water droplets on a glass surface
- A transparent glass globe holding a miniature forest
- A sunbeam through grass-bokeh
- ONE wind-turbine silhouette at the horizon

Foreground crisp + background bokeh. One motif, repeated with photographic depth — never five motifs piled on.

**Raster required:** plants / leaves / water droplets / solar panels / green-tech photography. Faking with CSS gradients reads as generic green SaaS marketing. Before drawing, follow the [**Raster requirements**](../prototype.md#raster-requirements--when-svg-will-not-deliver-the-genre) decision tree.

**Forbidden imagery:** AI-stock "hands cradling sapling in soil"; cartoon recycle-arrow ouroboros; flat-vector leaf clipart; dolphin/butterfly stock art (that's parent Aero — wrong motif); photo of a child holding a globe; "100% Natural" rosette badges in stamp-ink red.

## Voice register

Warm-corporate-optimistic with a stewardship register. Full sentences, contractions allowed, present-tense claims.

- "Better for your home. Better for the planet."
- "Made with naturally derived ingredients."
- "Cleans tough. Treats Earth gently."

Never terse-technical, never edgy-irreverent. Innocent Smoothies' gentle wink is the upper bound — anything snarkier reads as 2020s DTC, wrong era.

Typography is humanist-sans (Frutiger, Myriad Pro, Segoe UI, Lucida Grande) — geometric sans (Inter, Poppins, Geist, Nunito, Comfortaa) is an instant tell.

## Failure mode

A flat emerald-green page with a single Lucide leaf icon and Inter 16 body = AI Eco cosplay. Four reliable tells of the cheap version:

1. Pasting in AI stock "hand with sapling" instead of macro foliage
2. Forgetting the sky-blue half of the palette (pure-green-only)
3. Swapping Aero's dolphins for flat-vector leaf clipart
4. Using a geometric sans (Inter/Poppins/Geist/Nunito/Comfortaa)

Real Eco is dewy macro-photography as a plate + ONE chosen Eco motif + humanist sans + the BLUE retained at the top + glass chrome with the 1px inner-white highlight + warm-green-tinted shadows.

## Best for

- Sustainability and clean-energy brands
- Cleaning and personal-care product pages
- Organic food and grocery
- Climate-tech and carbon-offset landing pages
- Eco-tourism and national-parks microsites
- Charity / NGO 2008-revival campaigns
- Kids' nature-learning apps
- Anywhere the brief is "earnest stewardship of nature with a hopeful future" and you need the dewy-leaf-on-glass feeling without the parent Aero's gadget-tech connotation
