# Restrained AI marketing

A `(shell + style + voice)` bundle for **AI-tooling and modern-SaaS marketing pages** that lean on restrained-hairline minimalism instead of marketing flash.

## Picks

- **Shell:** `hero-stack` — read `shell-hero-stack.md`
- **Style:** `restrained-hairline` — read `style-restrained-hairline.md` (currently bundled mainly with three-column-app and centered-column; this recipe extends it into hero-stack marketing)
- **Aesthetic:** *(none — the discipline is the identity)*
- **Voice:** declarative product-truth in lowercase or sentence case, restrained. Optional editorial-serif accent on the hero headline (Geist + Martina, Inter + GT Sectra, Söhne + Tiempos). No marketing adjectives, no superlatives.

## Pattern

- Off-white or near-white surface (`#FAFAFA` / `oklch(99% 0.004 250)`); not pure white
- Hero with restrained typographic headline — sans-serif workhorse OR sans-serif + one serif accent word for editorial tone
- Hairline dividers between sections (`oklch(90% 0.005 250)`)
- Product mockups as cards with hairline borders + single soft shadow (`0 1px 0 oklch(0% 0 0 / 0.04)`)
- Single accent colour reserved for primary CTA only
- Generous whitespace, section padding `120–180px`
- Logo wall as monochrome marks
- Feature explanations as 2-3 sentence paragraphs, never bullet-soup
- Code blocks (if any) in mono with hairline border, no syntax-highlighter neon

## Best for

AI-tooling marketing pages, modern-SaaS landings, developer-product marketing where "we don't shout" is the posture, agency portfolio landing pages, premium-fintech marketing, anywhere the brand reads as "we know what we're doing and we don't need flash."

## What distinguishes this from existing recipes

- `linear-product-ui` is the three-column app surface — this is the hero-stack marketing for that posture.
- `readcv-portfolio` is the personal centered-column site — this is the company-marketing hero variant.
- `aurora-marketing` leads with atmosphere; this leads with restraint.
