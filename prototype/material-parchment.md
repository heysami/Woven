# Parchment / Vellum (animal hide, premium document) (material)


**Tag:** material-parchment
  ·  **Family:** analog  ·  **Category:** paper  ·  **Surface:** matte  ·  **Transparency:** opaque

A matte surface that reacts to light: no specular but visible thickness and deforms: yes — curls dramatically at corners.

**Common implementation mistakes (avoid these)**

- uniform colour (parchment is naturally splotchy)
- perfect rectangle (parchment has irregular hand-cut edges)

**Pairs with** (prototype slugs)

- `aesthetic-dark-academia`
- `aesthetic-steampunk`
- `aesthetic-defi-cosmic (achievement certificates)`
